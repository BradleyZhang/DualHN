运动中的墨滴夺得尼康小世界摄影大赛头奖

2026-10-29
  • 原文 Ink droplets in motion snag top prize in Nikon Small World contest

A 25-year-old German experimental photographer has won the 2026 Nikon Small World photomicrography competition with a striking image of droplets of ink being deposited on paper by an inkjet printer. Ole Bielfeldt beat out 1,802 other entries hailing from 69 different countries.

一位25岁的德国实验摄影师，用一张极具冲击力的照片赢得了2026年尼康Small World显微摄影比赛。这张照片捕捉到了墨水滴通过喷墨打印机滴落在纸张上的过程。奥勒·比尔费尔特从来自69个不同国家的1,802份参赛作品中脱颖而出。

Bielfeldt became interested in photomicrography during the COVID-19 lockdown after discovering an old microscope in his parents’ attic. He figured out how to build an adaptor out of a tube and some wood to connect the microscope to his father’s camera and taught himself the art through trial and error, building custom tools as needed. He documents how he captures transformative images of everyday objects with behind-the-scenes videos posted to macrofying, his photography and videography brand.

Bielfeldt 在 COVID-19 防疫封锁期间 对显微摄影 产生兴趣，后 在父母阁楼 里 发现 了 旧显微镜。他 设计 出 一种 由 管子 和 木头 制成 的 适配器 将 显微镜 与 父亲 的 相机 连接 并 通过 试错 自学 这 门 艺术 ， 必要 时 自行 制作 工具。他 documents 记录 他 如何 以 变革性 的 方式 捕捉 日常 物品 的 图像 并 将 幕后 视频 发布 到 macrofying， 这是 他的 摄影 和 摄像 品牌。

It was Bielfeldt’s curiosity about the inner workings of inkjet printers that inspired his winning entry, but capturing the action of ink drops being deposited on paper was a challenge, given that the process happens so quickly. The droplets, for instance, moved faster than the camera’s shutter speed. Bielfeldt’s solution was to deconstruct the printer so that the paper moved under a stationary (rather than moving) printhead, and then take the image in a dark room, using a flash to serve as the shutter, thereby freezing the ink drops mid-air. There was no need for pricey equipment. Bielfeldt does not use AI in his images, preferring to rely on traditional techniques.

比尔费尔德特对喷墨打印机内部工作原理的好奇心激发了他获奖作品的灵感，但捕捉墨滴沉积在纸上的动作颇具挑战，因为这一过程发生得极快。例如，墨滴的移动速度超过了相机的快门速度。比尔费尔德特的解决方案是拆解打印机，使纸张在固定（而非移动）的打印头下方移动，然后在暗室中利用闪光灯充当快门进行拍摄，从而将半空中的墨滴定格。这不需要昂贵的设备。比尔费尔德特的作品不使用 AI，他更倾向于依赖传统技术。

“Ole Bielfeldt’s winning image embodies what makes Nikon Small World so powerful: the intersection of microscope imaging, ingenuity, and artistry,” said Eric Flem, a spokesperson for Nikon Instruments. “By using a microscope objective in a different way, he transformed a familiar object into something altogether new and previously unseen. It is a reminder that the microscope is not only a scientific tool but also a creative one, capable of expanding how we see and imagine the world around us.”

“Ole Bielfeldt 的获胜图像体现了为什么 Nikon Small World 如此强大：显微镜成像、独创性和艺术性之间的交汇。” 表示 Eric Flem，Nikon Instruments 的一位发言人。“通过以不同的方式使用显微镜目标，他将一个熟悉的物体转变为完全新且以前从未见过的东西。它提醒我们，显微镜不仅仅是一项科学工具，也是一个创造性工具，能够扩大我们看待和想象周围世界的视野。”

Founded in 1974, the Nikon Small World competition celebrates its 52nd anniversary this year. In 2011, the company added the Small World in Motion competition (see the 2026 winner here). Both are meant “to showcase the beauty and complexity of things seen through the light microscope”—one through still photographs, the other through photovideography, taking advantage of all the technological advances that now allow users to make movies or time-lapse images through the microscope.

成立于 1974 年的 Nikon Small World 比赛，今年迎来其第 52 个周年。2011 年，该公司增加了 Small World in Motion 比赛（见这里的2026 winner here）。两者都意在“展示通过光学显微镜所看到的事物的美与复杂性”——一个通过静止照片，另一个通过 photovideography，利用所有技术进步，这些进步如今使用户能够通过显微镜制作电影或定时摄影图像。
