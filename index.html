After our reports to affected companies, all have had the script removed (on November 24th or 25th) ahead of Black Friday. We observed it in action on one of the five websites: Lascana, where it sent our test card’s details to a 3rd party. We believe the script was built to take a card number, expiry and security code, along with a user’s name, address, phone and email, and send them to malefactors as a user entered their payment credentials. It did this before the store checked the payment, so a card could be taken even if the order was declined or cancelled, and nothing would look wrong to the shopper.
