Across Google and our early access partners, Argon is quickly becoming the preferred model for engineers tackling long-horizon software engineering.

在 Google 内部及我们的早期访问合作伙伴中，Argon 正迅速成为工程师应对长周期软件工程任务的首选模型。

Argon brings major improvements in spatial reasoning, mathematical precision, and visual coding compared to previous Gemini models.

Argon 与之前的 Gemini 模型相比，在空间推理、数学精度和视觉编码方面带来了重大改进。

Argon built a highly detailed model of a Pagoda using multi-agent visual critique loops in Antigravity. The model autonomously sculpted a multi-building temple complex with photorealistic architecture, foliage and lighting in Blender.

Argon 在 Antigravity 中使用多代理视觉评审循环构建了一个高度详细的 Pagoda 模型。该模型在 Blender 中自主雕刻出包含逼真建筑、植被和照明的多建筑寺庙群。

Argon turned pictures of a real desk into a playable miniature racing game using a multi-agent loop in Antigravity. The desk objects, room and race track were modeled and collision-checked using Blender MCP, textured with Nano Banana, and wired with physics, custom shaders, and car engine audio from Gemini Omni Flash in Three.js.

Argon 将真实书桌的照片使用 Antigravity 中的 multi-agent loop 变成了可玩的微型赛车游戏。书桌物体、房间和赛道使用 Blender MCP 进行建模和碰撞检查，使用 Nano Banana 进行纹理处理，并使用 Three.js 中来自 Gemini Omni Flash 的物理、自定义着色器和汽车引擎音频进行布线。

Argon in Antigravity wrote Python scripts to procedurally sculpt, rig, animate, and PBR-bake a complex 3D game using multi-agent visual critique loops then wired it for the web in Three.js with GLSL shaders, shatter physics, and a single-player AI.

Argon in Antigravity 用 Python 脚本编程，程序化雕刻、rigging、动画并 PBR-bake 一个复杂的 3D 游戏，使用 multi-agent visual critique loops，随后将其布线至 Three.js，配合 GLSL 着色器、碎裂物理和 single-player AI。

With zero external libraries and 100% pure Python, Argon wrote its own 3D ray-tracing engine with a custom video tool and audio mixer to build and document a sunlit library from scratch, producing, narrating, and assembling this video with Gemini 3.8 TTS voiceover and music by Lyria 3.5.

在零外部库且 100% 纯 Python 的环境下，Argon 结合自定义的视频工具和音频混音器，编写了自家的 3D 光线追踪引擎，从零开始构建并记录了一个阳光充足的 library，并使用 Gemini 3.8 TTS 旁白和 Lyria 3.5 音乐制作、配音并合成了这段视频。

Starting from a single Nano Banana image in Antigravity, Argon drives Blender MCP in an autonomous loop, aligning camera grids, sculpting Gothic architecture, and staging audio-reactive lighting, to create a finished 3D scene.

从 Antigravity 中的一张 Nano Banana 图像出发，Argon 在自主循环中驱动 Blender MCP，对齐相机网格、雕刻哥特式建筑并搭建音频响应式灯光，最终生成一个完整的 3D 场景。

Argon combines frontier reasoning, deep visual perception, expanded domain knowledge, and stronger writing capabilities across legal, finance, science, and coding to handle high-stakes professional workflows.

氩（Argon）将前沿推理、深度视觉感知、扩展领域知识以及更强大的法律、金融、科学和编程写作能力相结合，能够高效处理高风险的专业工作流程。

At Isomorphic Labs, the team is using Argon as a partner to accelerate their drug discovery and design research workflows. Argon is able to quickly synthesize complex PDFs and patents to extract pertinent information, reducing a process that typically takes weeks into hours.

在Isomorphic Labs，该团队正在利用Argon作为合作伙伴来加速药物发现和设计研究工作流。Argon能够快速合成复杂的PDF和专利以提取相关信息，将原本需要数周才能完成的流程缩短至数小时。

In Gemini App, Argon takes an unstructured folder full of project context and formats it into a clean proposal deck for a client using a custom skill. Gemini then drafts and emails the deck to a client while creating an automated workflow for all future incoming client requests with a scheduled task.

在 Gemini App 中，Argon 将充满项目上下文的非结构化文件夹格式化为面向客户端的干净提案答辩，并使用自定义技能。Gemini 随后为客户端起草并发送电子邮件，同时为所有未来传入的客户端请求创建一个自动化工作流程，采用计划任务的方式。

See how Argon synthesizes ~50 GB of Airborne LiDAR scans, GIS room vectors, Library of Congress CAD drawings, and NPS archives to reconstruct 3D models of Chaco Canyon, turning fragmented archaeological records into explorable digital heritage sites.

了解阿尔贡如何合成约 50 GB 的航空激光雷达扫描数据、GIS 房间矢量数据、国会图书馆 CAD 绘图以及国家公园服务档案数据，重建查科峡谷的 3D 模型，并将零散的考古记录转化为可探索的数字遗产网站。

Watch Argon 将从3 个开源仓库综合数据，打造一个医疗学习工具，在其中你可以剥开解剖层次、检查临床元数据，并在人类身体的3D 探索中触发生物力学。

Argon is built to be an active research partner, moving beyond literature search to help scientists, mathematicians and researchers formulate, stress-test, and prove non-obvious hypotheses.

Argon 旨在成为一款活跃的研究伙伴，超越文献检索的范围，帮助科学家、数学家和研究人员构建、压力测试并证明非显而易见的假设。这使得研究人员能够更高效地推动科学发现的进程。

By bringing together advanced visual-semantic reasoning, deep physical understanding, and autonomous code generation, the model introduces a new set of capabilities across the robotics stack.

通过整合高级视觉-语义推理、深度物理理解和自主代码生成，该模型在机器人堆栈中引入了一套新的功能。

To demonstrate these capabilities in action, we tested Argon’s ability to learn an out of distribution physical skill simply by watching. In an internal test, Argon watched a raw video of a human playing cornhole, mapped the spatial coordinates of the environment, and calculated the required whole-body joint movements. It then autonomously built the simulation code. When transferred onto Apptronik’s Apollo humanoid, the robot quickly sank seven consecutive shots in a real-world trial.

为了在实际应用中展示这些能力，我们测试了 Argon 仅通过观看就能学习分布外物理技能的能力。在一项内部测试中，Argon 观看了人类玩 cornhole 游戏的原始视频，映射了环境的空间坐标，并计算了所需的全身关节运动。随后，它自主构建了仿真代码。当移植到 Apptronik 的 Apollo 人形机器人上后，该机器人在现实世界的试验中连续七次投掷命中。
