All the sites we contacted took action; but the larger consumer web ecosystem should be prepared to mitigate these attack vectors 我们联系的所有网站均已采取行动；但更广泛的消费级 Web 生态系统应做好准备，以缓解这些攻击向量。 A card-skimming script was planted on the online stores of Lascana, Samsonite, American Tourister, Revolve and FWRD in the week before Black Friday. After our reports to affected companies, all have had the script removed (on November 24th or 25th) ahead of Black Friday. We observed it in action on one of the five websites: Lascana, where it sent our test card’s details to a 3rd party. We believe the script was built to take a card number, expiry and security code, along with a user’s name, address, phone and email, and send them to malefactors as a user entered their payment credentials. It did this before the store checked the payment, so a card could be taken even if the order was declined or cancelled, and nothing would look wrong to the shopper.
