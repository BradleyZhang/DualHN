![Line chart, September 2024 to August 2026. Google results showing an AI Overview rise from 5.9% to 61.4%, while results showing a featured snippet fall from 9.0% to 0.4%.](https://revscope.co/_astro/search-features-2024-2026.BZl2SRMo_Z24aVWv.webp)

> Google's AI answers are taking clicks from websites in the search results. In a randomized experiment, researchers hid AI Overviews from some US desktop users. Comparing them with users who still saw Overviews shows that an Overview cuts clicks to outside websites by about 40%. Those Overviews now appear on more than half the US keywords DataForSEO tracks, up from 6% two years ago. The industry's answer, GEO (generative engine optimization), has yet to show a way to compensate for the loss. While AI citations correlate with the same kind of link profile that goes with higher Google rankings, in March AI assistants sent websites just 0.3% of their traffic.

Google 的 AI 答案正在从搜索结果中的网站抢走点击。在一项随机实验中，研究人员对某些美国桌面用户隐藏了 AI Overviews。将这些用户与仍然看到 Overviews 的用户进行比较，结果表明，一个 Overview 会使站外网站的点击量减少约 40%。如今，这些 Overviews 出现在 DataForSEO 跟踪的美国关键词中，覆盖率超过一半，而两年前这一比例为 6%。行业给出的答案是 GEO（生成式引擎优化），但至今仍未找到弥补这一损失的方法。尽管 AI 引用与通常与更高 Google 排名相关联的同类链接画像存在相关性，但在 3 月，AI 助手为网站带来的流量仅占网站总流量的 0.3%。

> Search engine optimization (SEO) still decides whether you can be found, but where an AI answer appears, a ranking brings 40% fewer visits than it used to. Generative engine optimization (GEO), the practice of getting your content cited in AI-written answers, looks mostly like SEO plus two additions: being present on the third-party sites AI answers draw from, and writing pages that are easy to quote. We found little evidence for any other tactic.

搜索引擎优化 (SEO) 仍决定你是否能被发现，但当 AI 回答出现时，排名带来的访问量比以前少了 40 %。生成引擎优化 (GEO) 是将你的内容在 AI 生成的答案中被引用的实践，整体上大体相当于 SEO 加上两项补充：在 AI 答案中引用的第三方网站上有所存在，以及编写易于引用的页面。我们发现几乎没有其他策略的证据。

> ## Google answers first

## Google 优先回答

> An AI Overview is a generated summary that Google places above the ordinary results, with links to the pages it drew on. In DataForSEO's monthly panel of 20 million keywords, the share of results showing one climbed from 5.93% in September 2024 to 61.43% in August 2026. The chart at the top of this post shows that climb. Over the same period the featured snippet, the quoted passage Google used to show at the top of a page, fell from 8.96% to 0.44%, and the discussions and forums block fell from 21.13% to 7.42%. Video results rose from 9.67% to 19.98%, though that is down from a peak of 31.05% in October 2025, and local map listings rose from 19.98% to 25.99%. DataForSEO's dataset covers 190 million keywords, 165 million AI answers, and 1.1 billion backlinks, mostly for US English searches. Pew Research Center's data gives an independent check. Pew logged the searches of 900 US adults in March 2025, re-ran them in April, and found summaries on 18% of them. DataForSEO's figures for those months are 14.67% and 17.63%, close to Pew's.

AI Overview 是 Google 放置在普通结果上方的生成摘要，并附带其引用页面的链接。在 DataForSEO 的 monthly panel of 20 million keywords 中，显示 AI Overview 的结果占比从 2024 年 9 月的 5.93% 上升至 2026 年 8 月的 61.43%。本帖顶部的图表展示了这一增长趋势。

在同一时期，featured snippet（Google 过去用于在页面顶部展示的引用段落）的占比从 8.96% 下降至 0.44%，discussions and forums block 的占比从 21.13% 下降至 7.42%。Video results 从 9.67% 上升至 19.98%，尽管这一数值低于 2025 年 10 月的峰值 31.05%；local map listings 从 19.98% 上升至 25.99%。

DataForSEO 的数据集涵盖 190 million 个关键词、165 million 个 AI 答案和 1.1 billion 个 backlinks，主要针对 US English 搜索。Pew Research Center 的数据提供了独立验证。Pew 记录了 900 名美国成年人在 2025 年 3 月的搜索，并在 4 月重新运行了这些搜索，发现 18% 的搜索显示了摘要。DataForSEO 在这两个月的数据分别为 14.67% 和 17.63%，与 Pew 的数据接近。

> Low-volume keywords aren't safer either. In the third quarter of 2026, keywords with 201 to 300 monthly searches showed Overviews 58.59% of the time, against 48.62% for those above 600. A separate dataset, from June to September 2026, covers difficulty and price. The easiest keywords showed Overviews 60.64% of the time and the hardest 53.76%. Keywords costing advertisers more than $5 a click were at 49.55% and those under 25 cents at 50.54%, with a low of 42.63% between $0.51 and $1.00. More than half the result pages have no price data and show Overviews 60.06% of the time. The rate does vary by industry. Health is at 69.68% and finance at 68.80%, while apparel is at 37.48%, dining at 29.37%, and real estate at 22.27%.

低搜索量关键词也并非更安全。2026 年第三季度，月搜索量在 201 到 300 次之间的关键词触发 AI 概览的比例为 58.59%，而月搜索量超过 600 次的关键词这一比例为 48.62%。另一份涵盖 2026 年 6 月至 9 月、包含难度和价格数据的数据集显示：竞争难度最低的关键词触发 AI 概览的比例为 60.64%，最难的为 53.76%。单次点击成本超过 5 美元的关键词触发比例为 49.55%，低于 0.25 美元的为 50.54%，而在 0.51 美元到 1.00 美元区间的关键词触发比例最低，为 42.63%。超过一半的结果页面没有价格数据，其触发 AI 概览的比例为 60.06%。不同行业的触发率差异较大：医疗健康行业为 69.68%，金融行业为 68.80%，而服装行业为 37.48%，餐饮行业为 29.37%，房地产行业为 22.27%。

> Queries are also getting longer, especially on desktop. Over a year, queries of five or more words grew from 29.3% to 34.8% of unique desktop queries and from 29.6% to 31.9% on mobile. Three and four word searches are still the most common.

查询语句也在变长，尤其是在桌面上。过去一年里，五个或多词的查询占桌面查询的唯一占比从29.3%增长到34.8%，而在移动端则从29.6%增长到31.9%。三、四字的查询仍然是最常见的。

> ## What happens to the clicks

## 点击都会经历什么

> The strongest evidence is an experiment. Saharsh Agarwal and Ananya Sen gave US desktop Chrome users an extension that put each of them at random into a group. One group saw Google as usual, and another had AI Overviews hidden. The researchers then watched two weeks of real searches in January and February 2026. On searches that would show an Overview, the group that could see them averaged 0.37 clicks to outside websites per search, against 0.62 for the group that couldn't, which the authors report as a 39.8% reduction. Searches ending with no outbound click went from 54% to 73%. Clicks on ads didn't change, which suggests Google takes care not to cannibalize its own revenue. Across all searches, with and without an Overview, the authors put the loss at roughly 18.5% of the organic clicks a site gets from Google. The authors expect the loss to grow as Overviews appear on more searches. This is a randomized controlled trial, the most reliable kind of study for separating cause from correlation or coincidence. The effect was statistically significant for informational searches. The navigational and transactional samples were too small to settle anything. This is a working paper, not yet peer reviewed, and it covers desktop only. A separate preregistered experiment by Wang and colleagues, with 1,100 people, also found that removing AI Overviews and AI Mode raised clicks to publishers.

最有力的证据来自一项实验。Saharsh Agarwal 和 Ananya Sen 向美国桌面 Chrome 用户提供了一个扩展程序，将他们随机分配到不同的组中。一组用户像往常一样看到 Google，另一组则隐藏了 AI Overviews。研究人员随后观察了 2026 年 1 月和 2 月期间两周的真实搜索情况。在会显示 AI Overviews 的搜索中，能够看到它们的组平均每次搜索点击外部网站 0.37 次，而无法看到的组为 0.62 次，作者报告这相当于减少了 39.8%。没有产生任何外部点击的搜索比例从 54% 上升到了 73%。广告点击量没有变化，这表明 Google 注意避免蚕食自身的收入。在所有搜索中（无论是否显示 AI Overviews），作者估计损失约为网站从 Google 获得的自然点击量的 18.5%。作者预计，随着 AI Overviews 出现在更多的搜索中，这一损失还会进一步扩大。

这是一项随机对照试验（randomized controlled trial），是区分因果关系与相关性或巧合的最可靠的研究类型。对于信息型搜索（informational searches），该效应在统计学上是显著的。导航型（navigational）和交易型（transactional）样本量太小，无法得出任何定论。这是一份工作论文（working paper），尚未经过同行评审，且仅涵盖桌面端。Wang 及其同事进行的另一项涉及 1,100 人的预先注册实验也发现，移除 AI Overviews 和 AI Mode 会增加出版商获得的点击量。

> Observational data points the same way. Pew found people clicked an ordinary result on 8% of visits to a results page with a summary and on 15% without one, and ended their browsing session on 26% against 16%. Pew's comparison isn't a controlled experiment, because the searches that draw a summary tend to be longer and phrased as questions.

观察性数据也指向了同样的方向。皮尤研究发现，当结果页面中存在摘要时，用户点击普通结果的比例为8%，而无摘要时则为15%。此外，在有摘要的情况下，用户结束浏览的比例为26%，无摘要时为16%。皮尤研究所的比较并非受控实验，因为产生摘要的搜索通常更长且以疑问句的形式呈现。

> Click-through rate (CTR) is the share of people shown a result who click it. Across more than 2,000 websites in DataForSEO's panel, the desktop CTR of the first organic result fell from 17.83% in September 2025 to 7.43% in August 2026, a drop of 58%. Mobile fell from 25.17% to 20.80%, a drop of 17%.

点击率（CTR）是指展示搜索结果后点击该结果的用户比例。根据 DataForSEO 面板中超过 2,000 个网站的数据，桌面端首个自然搜索结果的 CTR 从 2025 年 9 月的 17.83% 下降至 2026 年 8 月的 7.43%，跌幅达 58%。移动端 CTR 则从 25.17% 下降至 20.80%，跌幅为 17%。

> The click share for the Overview's cited links looks promising, but it comes from a model. Pew, which logged real browsing, saw people click a link inside a summary on just 1% of visits, against 8% for ordinary results. Eighteen months and a large change in the product separate the two measurements, so it is difficult to say which is closer to the truth.

Overview 中引用链接的点击份额看起来很有前景，但这一数据来自一个模型。记录了真实浏览行为的 Pew 发现，用户在摘要内部点击链接的访问占比仅为 1%，而普通结果的这一比例为 8%。两项测量相隔 18 个月，且期间产品经历了重大变更，因此很难说哪一个更接近事实真相。

Reddit 也出现在 Google 的普通结果前十名中，占报告追踪的结果的 32.15%，几乎是 Quora 的 4.11% 的近八倍。对此有两个限制。第一个限制是，这些回答来自 DataForSEO 收集的提示词，而报告并未解释它们是如何被选择的，也不清楚它们是否与人们真正想问的问题相符。第二个限制是，大型平台在普通结果中的表现也差不多：Pew 发现，Wikipedia、YouTube 和 Reddit 占据了 AI 摘要来源的 15%，也占了标准结果来源的 17%。尽管如此，一种流传已久的观点，即 AI 偏好 Reddit，在这些数据中得到了支持。

> ## GEO looks mostly like SEO

## GEO 看起来很像 SEO

> Google's position is that there are "no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary", beyond a page being indexed and eligible to show with a snippet. Since that is a company describing its own product, we looked at outside data on two questions. The first is which sites get considered, and the second is which page on a site gets picked.

Google 的立场是，除了页面被收录并有资格带摘要展示之外，“出现在 AI Overviews 或 AI Mode 中无需额外要求，也无需其他特殊优化”。由于这是 Google 对其自身产品的描述，我们查阅了外部数据来回答两个问题。第一是哪些站点会被纳入考虑，第二是站点中的哪个页面会被选中。

> ### Which sites: link profiles correlate with citations

### 哪些网站：link profiles 与 citations 相关

> A referring domain is another website that links to yours. DataForSEO measured how closely referring domains track AI citations using Spearman's correlation, a score where 0 means no relationship between two rankings and 1 means they match exactly. The score was 0.406 for AI Overviews and 0.377 for ChatGPT. Links marked as coming from user-generated content, such as forum posts, scored lower, at 0.342 and 0.314.

referring domain 是另一个链接到你的网站的网站。 DataForSEO 测量了referring domains 如何密切跟踪 AI citations 使用 Spearman's correlation， 0 表示两个排名之间没有关系， 1 表示完全匹配。 分数 为 AI Overviews 的 0.406 与 ChatGPT 的 0.377。 user-generated content 标记的链接， forum posts， 得分更低， 为 0.342 和 0.314。

> These are moderate relationships, and they don't show that links cause citations, since large, well-known sites tend to have more of everything. The samples also contain only domains cited at least once, so they can't tell you what it takes to get cited for the first time. A similar pattern shows up in ordinary rankings: domains ranking first had a median of 111 referring domains against 85 for positions 4 to 10, with heavy overlap between the groups. For AI Overviews, the median cited domain had 282 referring domains on the easiest keywords and 1,571 on the hardest, after the report excluded giant platforms like YouTube and Amazon and any domain with fewer than 50 referring domains. Small sites weren't measured at all. The ranges are also wide, at 518 to 5,765 for the middle half of domains on the hardest keywords, so sites with very different link profiles get cited.

这些是中等程度的相关关系，并不能证明链接会导致引用，因为大型知名网站往往在所有方面都拥有更多。样本中也只包含至少被引用过一次的域名，因此无法告诉你首次获得引用需要什么条件。类似的模式也出现在普通排名中：排名第一的域名的 referring domains（引荐域名）中位数为 111，而第 4 至第 10 位为 85，两组之间存在大量重叠。对于 AI Overviews，在报告排除了 YouTube 和 Amazon 等大型平台以及引荐域名少于 50 个的域名后，被引用域名的引荐域名中位数在最简单关键词上为 282，在最难关键词上为 1,571。小型网站完全未被纳入测量。范围也很宽：在最难关键词上，中间一半域名的范围为 518 至 5,765，因此链接构成差异极大的网站也会被引用。

> ### Which page: cited pages often don't rank for the query

### 哪个页面：被引用的页面常不因查询而排名

> Ranking for a query and being cited for it are drifting apart. Ahrefs found 76% of pages cited in AI Overviews ranked in the top 10 for that query in [July 2025](https://ahrefs.com/blog/search-rankings-ai-citations), and about 38% in [March 2026](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-citations-top-10). The two studies differ in sample, parsing, and the Gemini model behind the Overviews, so the size of that fall isn't a clean measurement. One possible mechanism is what Google calls query fan-out: its documentation says these features may issue "multiple related searches across subtopics and data sources". A page could then be cited because it ranks for a related question the reader never typed. Ahrefs offers this only as a possibility, and its earlier study tested the idea and found that the data didn't support it.

针对某个查询的排名和被引用正在逐渐脱钩。Ahrefs 发现，在 [2025 年 7 月](https://ahrefs.com/blog/search-rankings-ai-citations) 的研究中，AI Overviews 引用的页面中有 76% 在该查询中排名前 10，而 [2026 年 3 月](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-citations-top-10) 的研究中这一比例约为 38%。这两项研究在样本、解析方式以及 Overviews 背后的 Gemini 模型方面存在差异，因此无法精确衡量这一降幅的大小。一种可能的机制是 Google 所谓的 query fan-out（查询发散）：其文档指出，这些功能可能会"跨子主题和数据源发起多次相关搜索"。这样一来，一个页面可能因为其在读者从未输入过的相关问题上的排名而被引用。Ahrefs 仅将其作为一种可能性提出，其早期的研究对此进行了验证，发现数据并不支持该假设。

> The most careful test of page-level tactics we found is a study by the SEO software company SISTRIX, published in October 2026. It paired 48,527 pages cited by ChatGPT, AI Mode, or AI Overviews with uncited pages from the same site, which removes the site's overall strength from the comparison. In the table, a factor of 2.7 means pages with the feature were cited 2.7 times as often as pages without it.

我们发现的最为严谨的页面级策略测试，是 SEO 软件公司 SISTRIX 于 2026 年 10 月发布的一项研究。它将 48,527 个被 ChatGPT、AI Mode 或 AI Overviews 引用的页面，与来自同一站点但未被引用的页面进行配对，从而消除了站点整体实力对比较结果的影响。在表格中，2.7 的系数意味着具有该特征的页面被引用的次数是没有该特征页面的 2.7 倍。

> An experiment in the paper that gave GEO its name points the same way as SISTRIX's correlations. Aggarwal and colleagues rewrote web pages and measured how much of a generated answer drew on them. On their 10,000-query benchmark, adding quotations raised a page's visibility by about 41%, adding statistics by about 31%, and citing sources by about 27%, while keyword stuffing lowered it by about 8%, all on their main measure, the position-weighted share of the answer's words. Lower-ranked pages gained the most: citing sources lifted a page ranked fifth by 115% and cut the top-ranked page by 30%. They also tried Perplexity, an AI search product, on 200 samples, uploading the source text as files rather than letting it search. The gains on that measure were smaller, at about 21% for quotations and 9% for statistics. The main test used GPT-3.5 reading the top five search results, which is well behind today's products, so the percentages are dated even if the direction still holds.

为 GEO 命名的那篇论文中有一个实验，其结论与 SISTRIX 的相关性分析指向同一方向。Aggarwal 及其同事重写了网页内容，并测量生成的答案在多大程度上引用了这些页面。在他们那套包含 10,000 个查询的基准测试上，加入引文使页面的可见度提升约 41%，加入统计数据提升约 31%，标注来源提升约 27%，而堆砌关键词则使其下降约 8%——以上都是基于他们的主要衡量指标，即按答案中词语位置加权的占比。排名靠后的页面收益最大：标注来源使排名第五的页面提升了 115%，却使排名第一的页面下降了 30%。他们还在 200 个样本上测试了 AI 搜索产品 Perplexity，方式是上传源文本作为文件，而不是让它自行检索。在该指标下收益较小，引文约为 21%，统计数据为 9%。主要测试使用的是让 GPT-3.5 读取前五个搜索结果，这已经远远落后于当下的产品，因此即便方向依然成立，这些百分比也已过时。

> The most debated shortcut has nothing behind it so far. llms.txt is a proposed file that tells AI systems what a site contains. SE Ranking analyzed nearly 300,000 domains and found no correlation with citations. SISTRIX also concluded there is "no measurable effect", though it cautions that domains with the file differ from others in many ways, so this part of its data doesn't have the same-site pairing. Google says "you don't need to create new machine readable files, AI text files, or markup" for its AI features. The data agrees about the files, though SISTRIX's FAQ structured data result suggests some markup may help.

目前最受争议的捷径背后仍没有任何实质内容。llms.txt 是一份提议中的文件，用于告诉 AI 系统网站包含的内容。SE Ranking 分析了近 300,000 个域名，未发现与引用的相关性。SISTRIX 也得出结论称不存在“可衡量的效果”，但它提醒，拥有该文件的域名在很多方面与其他域名不同，因此其数据的这部分不具备同站点配对条件。Google 表示，针对其 AI 功能，“你不需要创建新的机器可读文件、AI 文本文件或 markup”。关于这些文件，数据观点一致，不过 SISTRIX 的 FAQ structured data 结果表明，某些 markup 可能有帮助。

> ## AI sends little traffic, but it is growing and may convert well

## AI 带来的流量很少,但正在增长,且转化率可能较高

> AI assistants sent 0.28% of total traffic across 74,752 websites in March 2026, according to Ahrefs. That was about 3.5 million visitors, against 345 million from Google, or roughly a hundred to one. Ahrefs describes it as small but growing fast. The true figure is somewhat higher because some visits, especially from mobile apps, get counted as direct traffic. The same Ahrefs article reports that on its own site, AI search visitors were 0.5% of visitors and 12.1% of signups. Ahrefs sells SEO software, so its audience may be unusually likely to use AI tools, and one site proves little. Still, on that site an AI visitor was about 24 times as likely to sign up as the average visitor. Some of the value never shows up as traffic. People read an AI answer, form an opinion, and never click.

> ## What to do about it

如何应对

> 1. **Keep the fundamentals.** Pages must be indexable and eligible for a snippet. Referring domains correlated with citations more strongly than user-generated links did, and higher-ranking domains had moderately more of them, so links from other sites remain a reasonable investment, even though neither relationship proves cause.
> 2. **Find out who gets cited on your topics, per platform.** Check your important queries in AI Overviews and ChatGPT and note the sources. Being described accurately there may matter as much as your own page. Take part in the communities those answers cite, such as Reddit, so that you are mentioned there.
> 3. **Write pages worth quoting.** Answer related questions directly, add original data and named sources, and use clear headings. If you run an editorial site, show when the page was last updated.
> 4. **Skip the shortcuts.** An llms.txt does no harm but has shown no benefit, AI-specific markup isn't needed, and keyword stuffing lowered visibility in the GEO paper's test.
> 5. **Judge the work by revenue.** With fewer clicks to go around, it matters more which ones pay. Tie each channel to the sales it produces rather than to its rank or its visit count.

1. **保持基础。** 页面必须可索引且有资格生成摘要。推荐域名与引用的相关性比用户生成的链接更强，排名较高的域名拥有适度更多的此类链接，因此来自其他站点的链接仍然是一项合理的投资，尽管这两种关系都未证明因果关系。

2. **找出在每个平台上谁在你的主题下被引用。** 在 AI Overviews 和 ChatGPT 中检查你的重要查询并记录来源。在那里被准确描述可能与你自己的页面同样重要。参与那些答案引用的社区（例如 Reddit），以便在其中提到你。

3. **写出值得被引用的页面。** 直接回答相关问题，添加原始数据和具名来源，并使用清晰的标题。如果你运营一个编辑型网站，展示页面的最后更新时间。

4. **跳过捷径。** llms.txt 虽无害但未显示任何益处，无需 AI 专属标记，且关键词堆砌在 GEO 论文的测试中降低了可见度。

5. **以收入评判工作。** 随着可获得的点击次数减少，哪些点击能带来收益变得更加重要。将每个渠道与其产生的销售额挂钩，而不是与其排名或访问量挂钩。

> The numbers above are mainly US English, and the products change quickly, so the 2025 figures may already be out of date. Nearly all the large datasets come from companies that sell SEO data or software, including DataForSEO, Ahrefs, SISTRIX, and SE Ranking, and we sell analytics. Almost everything about what earns a citation is correlation. Three findings hold up. Controlled experiments show fewer clicks to outside websites when Google's AI features are present, domains with more referring domains tend to be cited more often among those already cited, and we found no proven shortcut to being cited.

上面的数字主要是美国英语，而产品更新速度很快，因此2025年的数据可能已经过时了。几乎所有大型数据集都来自销售SEO数据或软件的公司，如DataForSEO、Ahrefs、SISTRIX和SE Ranking，而我们则专注于销售分析工具。关于什么才能获得引用，几乎所有内容都与相关性有关。三个结论显而易见：受控实验表明，当谷歌的AI功能启用时，点击外部网站的次数会减少；在已引用的域名中，拥有更多引用域的域名 tend 更常被引用；我们没有发现任何被引用的捷径。
