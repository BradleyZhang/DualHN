> [Nvidia](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/NVDA/) on Monday announced [Jensen Huang](https://www.cnbc.com/jensen-huang/) has been [reinstated as CEO](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director), effective immediately, a dramatic twist in a scandal that hammered the chipmaker's stock price and rocked the tech and [artificial intelligence](https://www.cnbc.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/) industries.

[Nvidia](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/NVDA/) 周一宣布，[Jensen Huang](https://www.cnbc.com/jensen-huang/) 已 [reinstated as CEO](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director)，effective immediately，这是一个在芯片制造商的股价震荡的丑闻中惊天转折，震动了科技和 [artificial intelligence](https://www.cnbc.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/) 行业。

> The board of directors said it unanimously voted to reinstate Huang following "a series of discussions and meetings" with him. The board added that it "received input from senior levels of the United States government" and that it is confident Huang's reinstatement "poses no national security risk."

董事会表示，在与黄进行了“一系列讨论和会议”后，一致投票决定恢复其职务。董事会补充说，它“收到了来自美国政府高层的意见”，并对黄的复职“不构成国家安全风险”充满信心。

> President [Donald Trump](https://www.cnbc.com/donald-trump/) [told CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/trump-nvidia-jensen-huang.html) on Monday that he had been speaking to Nvidia's board "directly and indirectly," and that Huang "did nothing wrong." Trump said he talked to Huang on Sunday and asked him to promise to "work hard."

总统 [唐纳德·特朗普](https://www.cnbc.com/donald-trump/) 周一 [告诉 CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/trump-nvidia-jensen-huang.html) 说，他一直直接和间接地与 Nvidia 董事会交谈，并且 Huang 「没有做错任何事」。特朗普说，他周日与 Huang 通了话，并要求他承诺「努力工作」。

> Huang [resigned](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-live-updates.html) from his role as chief executive earlier this month after a series of shocking reports about multiple extramarital affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/22/us/politics/alice-huang-china-spy-agency-nvidia.html) reported on Sunday that the woman, Alice Huang, was in contact with an officer of China's Ministry of State Security during at least part of the time that she was engaging in a relationship with Huang.

胡安 (Jensen Huang) 于本月早些时候辞职（https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-live-updates.html），此前他在 Nvidia 担任首席执行官的职位引发了一系列令人震惊的消息，涉及多起婚外情丑闻，包括与疑似中国间谍的一起。 《纽约时报》 (https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/22/us/politics/alice-huang-china-spy-agency-nvidia.html) 周日报道称，女性阿丽丝·胡安 (Alice Huang)，在她与胡安交往期间至少有一部分时间内，与中国国家安全部 (Ministry of State Security) 官员有联系。

> Shares of Nvidia closed up 31% following Huang's reinstatement. The stock had shed nearly 33% from the time Huang admitted to an extramarital affair on Nov. 8. The stock closed at $186.64 on Nov.6.

Nvidia 的股价在 Huang 被重新任命后收涨 31%。该股在 Huang 于 11月8日承认婚外情后下跌近 33%。该股于 11月6日收于 186.64 美元。

> "I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution," Huang said in a statement. "I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment. I want to thank the NVIDIA Board for its confidence and deliberation through this process."

我荣幸回归 NVIDIA，并领导世界上最具影响力的科技公司之一，当时正处于新工业革命的开端。黄在声明中说。我以最大的谦卑和更新的承诺承担这份责任。我想感谢 NVIDIA 董事会在整个过程中的信任与审慎。

> Senior government officials briefed Nvidia's board late last week and communicated that no national security laws were violated, according to sources familiar with the discussion who asked to remain anonymous because they were not authorized to speak publicly. That update prompted the board to reach out to Huang, the people said.

去年晚些时候，几位高级政府官员向 Nvidia 的董事会 做了简报 并 表示 没有违反国家安全法律，据熟悉讨论情况且希望保持匿名，因为他们未获公开发言授权。此次更新促使董事会联系了 Huang 相关人士 表示。

> Huang and the board held their first conversations since his ouster on Saturday, and the group determined he would return as CEO during a second meeting on Sunday, the people said. Nvidia's cratering stock price played a role in accelerating those conversations.

知情人士称，黄与董事会在周六举行了他被免职以来的首次对话，董事会在周日的第二次会议上决定他将重新出任 CEO。Nvidia 股价的暴跌加速了这些对话。

> Trump celebrated Huang's return to Nvidia on Monday, writing in [a post on his social media site](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/117666141823845971) that the board made "the correct and proper decision" to reinstate him. Trump said the U.S. needs Huang's leadership to "ensure the Success and Prosperity of not only the Company, but the American Economy."

特朗普在周一庆祝黄（Huang）重返 Nvidia，并在[其社交媒体平台的一则帖子](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/117666141823845971)中写道，董事会做出复职的决定是"正确且恰当的决定"。特朗普表示，美国需要黄（Huang）的领导，以"确保不仅公司，而且美国经济的成功与繁荣"。

> He reiterated that the U.S. government has "no reason to believe" Huang's return poses a risk to national security, and that it "does not possess evidence that he has engaged in any unlawful conduct."

他重申，美国政府“没有理由认为”Huang 的返回对国家安全构成风险，并且该政府“没有证据表明他从事过任何违法行为”。

> "On a personal note, I have gotten to know Jensen, and he is a Great Patriot and National Treasure," Trump wrote.

就个人而言，我已经认识了 Jensen，他是一位伟大的爱国者和国家宝藏，特朗普写道。

> Several tech industry leaders, including [Elon Musk](https://www.cnbc.com/elon-musk/), CEO of [SpaceX](https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/SPCX/), Gavin Baker, managing partner and chief investment officer at Atreides Management, and Brad Gerstner, Altimeter Capital CEO, had been [actively lobbying](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/nvidia-investors-jensen-huang-return-board-letter.html) for Huang's return. Musk wrote in [a post on X](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124875939133751684?a=o6nENohXcBS7Mmq1J1w&s=20) on Monday that the board's "mistake has been corrected."

包括 SpaceX CEO [Elon Musk](https://www.cnbc.com/elon-musk/)、Atreides Management 管理合伙人兼首席投资官 Gavin Baker 以及 Altimeter Capital CEO Brad Gerstner 在内的多位科技行业领袖，一直在积极游说 Huang 回归董事会。Musk 周一在 X 上 [发文](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2124875939133751684?a=o6nENohXcBS7Mmq1J1w&s=20) 称，董事会的"错误已经纠正"。

> Nvidia did not immediately respond to CNBC's request for comment.

Nvidia 未及时回应 CNBC 的评论请求

> For weeks following Huang's ouster, the company's board maintained that it was [not considering](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang.html) Huang's reinstatement. It appointed Colette Kress, Nvidia's chief financial officer, as the chipmaker's interim CEO, who reiterated that position [in an interview with CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/nvidia-kress-jensen-huang-return.html) following Huang's exit. Kress will continue to serve as Nvidia's CFO, according to [a filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000090/nvda-20261122.htm) with the Securities and Exchange Commission.

在 Huang 被罢免后的数周里，公司董事会表示，其 [not considering](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang.html) Huang 的复职。它任命 Colette Kress 为 Nvidia 的首席财务官，接任芯片制造商的 interim CEO，她在 Huang 的离职后不久接受 CNBC 的采访时重申了这一立场。Kress 将继续担任 Nvidia 的 CFO，根据 [a filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000090/nvda-20261122.htm) 中来自 Securities and Exchange Commission 的文件。

> Nvidia's board formed an independent committee and hired the law firm [Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/nvidia-wachtell-lipton-huang-affair.html) to conduct an investigation into Huang and whether company secrets or national security were compromised. The board said Monday that "the committee's investigation will proceed to conclusion" and that Huang is "committed to cooperating with it."

Nvidia 董事会成立了一个独立委员会，并聘请律所 [Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/nvidia-wachtell-lipton-huang-affair.html) 对 Huang 以及公司机密或国家安全是否受到损害进行调查。董事会周一表示，“委员会的调查将推进至结论”，并且 Huang “承诺配合该调查”。

> "The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead NVIDIA, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," Nvidia's board chair, Harvey Jones, said in a statement. "We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the Company."

"董事会认为 Jensen 是领导 NVIDIA 的合适人选，他的复职符合我们股东、员工、合作伙伴和客户的最佳利益，" Nvidia 董事会主席 Harvey Jones 在一份声明中表示。"我们对 Jensen 有充分信心，并期待在他恢复公司领导职务之际与他合作。"

> Even with Huang back at the helm, Nvidia is facing increased scrutiny over its chip sales in Southeast Asia and China.

尽管黄仁勋重掌大权，英伟达仍因其在东南亚和中国的芯片销售而面临更大的审查。

> Lawmakers have been preparing for investigations into how Huang's affair with the alleged spy may have damaged U.S. AI dominance, influenced his positions on export controls, and impacted his close relationships with Trump and his Cabinet. Huang has been one of the loudest voices opposing strict export controls on advanced chips.

立法者一直在准备调查，Huang 与所谓间谍的婚外情可能如何损害了美国 AI 主导地位、如何影响了他对出口管制的立场、以及如何影响了他与特朗普及其内阁的密切关系。Huang 一直是反对对先进芯片实施严格出口管制的最强硬声音之一。

> "The reckless decision to bring Jensen Huang back as CEO does not change the fact that Mr. Huang, NVIDIA executives, and the company's board still owe Congress answers about the company's compliance with U.S. export controls," Sen. [Elizabeth Warren](https://www.cnbc.com/elizabeth-warren/), D-Mass., said in a statement Monday.

“鲁莽的决定将 Jensen Huang 重新请回 CEO，并不改变 Mr. Huang、 NVIDIA 高管和公司董事会仍欠 Congress 关于公司遵守 U.S. export controls 的答复的事实”，Sen. [Elizabeth Warren](https://www.cnbc.com/elizabeth-warren/), D-Mass., 说周一的一份声明

> Huang is due for a closed-door transcribed interview with the House Foreign Affairs panel and two other committees on Dec. 1. Nvidia's general counsel, Tim Teter, is scheduled to appear before the panels on Dec. 2. Those dates were set before Huang's return, and John Fisher, who represents Huang, did not comment on whether there are any changes in his plans to testify.

黄仁勋定于 12 月 1 日接受众议院外交事务委员会及另外两个委员会的闭门笔录访谈。Nvidia 总法律顾问 Tim Teter 计划于 12 月 2 日出席这些委员会。这些日期在黄仁勋返回之前就已确定，黄仁勋的代表 John Fisher 未就其作证计划是否有任何变化置评。

> Senate Banking Committee Chairman Tim Scott, R-S.C., said Monday that Huang will publicly testify on Dec. 3 as the sole witness during a hearing titled, "Protecting America's AI Advantage: Oversight of U.S. Export Controls."

参议院银行委员会主席蒂姆·斯科特（R-S.C.）周一表示，黄仁勋将于 12 月 3 日以唯一证人身份，在题为 "Protecting America's AI Advantage: Oversight of U.S. Export Controls"（保护美国的人工智能优势：对美国出口管制的监督）的听证会上公开作证。

> "Americans deserve answers about what NVIDIA and the Trump administration knew, when they knew it, and whether sufficient steps were taken to prevent sensitive technology from falling into the wrong hands," Sen. Andy Kim, D-N.J., a member of the Banking Committee, wrote in [a post on X](https://x.com/SenatorAndyKim/status/2124890579892854785?a=bbBTGcD8Qyh66IkB67M&s=20) on Monday.

“美国人应该得到关于 NVIDIA 和 特朗普政府知道什么、何时知晓，以及是否采取了充分措施防止敏感技术落入他人之手的答案，”新泽西州民主党参议员、银行委员会成员安迪·金（Sen. Andy Kim）周一在 [X 上的一篇帖子](https://x.com/SenatorAndyKim/status/2124890579892854785?a=bbBTGcD8Qyh66IkB67M&s=20) 中写道。
