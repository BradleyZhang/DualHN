> An ongoing cyberattack has reportedly struck major supermarket chains across Europe, disrupting refrigeration control systems. The attack — which has reportedly seen units set to continuously defrost at 50 degrees Celsius — is also apparently affecting deliveries and in-store pricing powered by electronic shelf labels, [*La Dépêche*](https://www.ladepeche.fr/2026/10/12/intermarche-leclerc-et-super-u-vises-par-une-cyberattaque-la-temperature-des-frigos-est-montee-jusqua-50-degres-des-degats-considerables-attendus-13596843.php) reports*.*

据报道，欧洲多家超市连锁店遭遇了一起持续性的网络攻击，导致制冷控制系统瘫痪。攻击过程中，设备 reportedly 持续在 50 摄氏度下除霜，同时还影响了由电子价签管理的配送和店内定价。[*La Dépêche*](https://www.ladepeche.fr/2026/10/12/intermarche-leclerc-et-super-u-vises-par-une-cyberattaque-la-temperature-des-frigos-est-montee-jusqua-50-degres-des-degats-considerables-attendus-13596843.php) 报道称。*

> According to the report, "several" major retailers have been targeted by a cyberattack, which has hit Danfoss-made refrigeration controls used by French supermarkets Intermarché, Leclerc, and Coopérative U. Indeed, the latter's president, Dominique Schelcher, has confirmed as of Monday morning that "A cyberattack has been affecting the refrigeration production facilities of several stores in our network since last night." Schelcher said teams had been mobilized to fix the issue and promised an investigation into the incident, which he described as "extremely serious."

据报道，几家主要零售商遭到了网络攻击，此次攻击影响了由丹佛斯生产的冷藏控制装置，这些装置被法国超市Intermarché、Leclerc和Coopérative U所使用。Coopérative U的总裁多米尼克·舍尔切尔（Dominique Schelcher）在周一早些时候证实：“从昨晚开始，我们网络中几家门店的制冷生产设施一直受到网络攻击。”舍尔切尔表示，已调动团队修复问题，并承诺对此次事件进行调查，他形容此事件为“极其严重”。
