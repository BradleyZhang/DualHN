> In the early 1960s, Richard Feynman gave a series of undergraduate lectures that were collected into a book called [the Feynman Lectures on Physics](https://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0465023827/ref=nosim/0sil8). Absent from the book was [a lecture](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mcD-5UfY1g0) Feynman gave on planetary motion, but a later finding of the notes enabled David Goodstein, a colleague of Feynman’s, to write a book about it: [Feynman’s Lost Lecture](https://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0393319954/ref=nosim/0sil8). From an excerpt of the book published in [a 1996 issue of Caltech’s Engineering & Science magazine](http://calteches.library.caltech.edu/563/2/Goodstein.pdf):

在 1960 年代早期，Richard Feynman 给出了一系列本科讲座，这些讲座被收录成一本名为 [the Feynman Lectures on Physics](https://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0465023827/ref=nosim/0sil8) 的书。然而，这本书中缺失了一篇关于行星运动的讲座，[a lecture](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mcD-5UfY1g0) 但随后对讲义的发现使得 David Goodstein，一位 Feynman 的同事，能够写出关于它的书籍：[Feynman’s Lost Lecture](https://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0393319954/ref=nosim/0sil8)。以下摘录来自 1996 年 《Caltech’s Engineering & Science》杂志的刊物（ http://calteches.library.caltech.edu/563/2/Goodstein.pdf）：

> > Feynman was a truly great teacher. He prided himself on being able to devise ways to explain even the most profound ideas to beginning students. Once, I said to him, “Dick, explain to me, so that I can understand it, why spin one-half particles obey Fermi-Dirac statistics.” Sizing up his audience perfectly, Feynman said, “I’ll prepare a freshman lecture on it.” But he came back a few days later to say, “I couldn’t do it. I couldn’t reduce it to the freshman level. That means we don’t really understand it.”

费曼是一位真正伟大的教师。他总是为自己能够设计出让初学者理解最深奥概念的方法而自豪。有一次，我对他说：“迪克，向我解释一下，让我能理解，为什么自旋为 1/2 的粒子遵循费米-狄拉克统计。”费曼准确判断了听众的水平，回答道：“我会准备一场关于这个主题的大一讲座。”但几天后他回来告诉我：“我做不到。我无法将它降低到大一学生的理解水平。这意味着我们并没有真正理解它。”

> [John Gruber writes](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2017/06/14/feynman-freshman-lecture) the simple explanations are the goal at Apple as well:

[John Gruber 写了](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2017/06/14/feynman-freshman-lecture) 简单的解释也是 Apple 的目标：

> > Engineers are expected to be able to explain a complex technology or product in simple, easily-understood terms not because the executive needs it explained simply to understand it, but as proof that the engineer understands it completely.

工程师之所以要能用简单易懂的语言解释复杂的技术或产品，并非因为高管需要简单的解释才能听懂，而是作为工程师彻底理解该技术的证明。

> Feynman was well known for simple explanations of scientific concepts that result a in deeper understanding of the subject matter: e.g. see Feynman explaining [how fire is stored sunshine](http://kottke.org/15/03/richard-feynman-fire-is-stored-sunshine), [rubber bands](http://kottke.org/13/09/richard-feynman-explains-rubber-bands), [how trains go around curves](http://kottke.org/13/04/why-dont-trains-need-differential-gears), and [magnets](http://kottke.org/10/02/richard-feynman-explains-magnets-sort-of). Critically, he’s also not shy about admitting when he doesn’t understand something…or, alternately, when scientists as a group don’t understand something. There’s the spin anecdote above and of [his explanation of magnets](http://kottke.org/10/02/richard-feynman-explains-magnets-sort-of), he says:

费曼以简单解释科学概念而闻名，这种解释带来对主题的更深入理解：例如，看看费曼如何解释 [how fire is stored sunshine](http://kottke.org/15/03/richard-feynman-fire-is-stored-sunshine), [rubber bands](http://kottke.org/13/09/richard-feynman-explains-rubber-bands), [how trains go around curves](http://kottke.org/13/04/why-dont-trains-need-differential-gears), 和 [magnets](http://kottke.org/10/02/richard-feynman-explains-magnets-sort-of). 关键在于，他也不吝啬承认自己某事不懂，或者，换一种说法，当科学家们作为一个群体时不明白某事。 上面的 spin 轶事以及他 [his explanation of magnets](http://kottke.org/10/02/richard-feynman-explains-magnets-sort-of) 的解释，他说道：

> > I really can’t do a good job, any job, of explaining magnetic force in terms of something else you’re more familiar with, because I don’t understand it in terms of anything else you’re more familiar with.

我真的无法用你更熟悉的任何事物来说好磁力的解释，因为我自己也并不理解磁力究竟是以什么为基础的。

> Feynman was also quoted as saying:

费曼也曾被引述说：

> > I think I can safely say that nobody understands quantum mechanics.

我认为我可以安全地说，没有人理解量子力学。

> Pretty interesting thing to hear from a guy who won a Nobel Prize for explaining quantum mechanics better than anyone ever had before. Even when he died in 1988 at the end of a long and fruitful careeer, a note at the top of [his blackboard](http://archives-dc.library.caltech.edu/islandora/object/ct1%3A483) read:

能从一个因以前所未有地更好地解释 quantum mechanics 而荣获 Nobel Prize 的人那里听到这话，颇为有趣。即使在他 1988 年辞世时，漫长而硕果累累的职业生涯已近尾声，他 [黑板](http://archives-dc.library.caltech.edu/islandora/object/ct1%3A483) 顶端的一张便条上写着：

> > What I cannot create, I do not understand.

> 我无法创造的，我便不理解。
