> In April, [Ecohustler disrupted Carnival Cruise's AGM](https://ecohustler.com/article/protesters-disrupt-top-corporate-polluter-carnival-corporation%27s-agm-in-london/) calling on the shareholders to reduce their company's pollution using straight forward and affordable methods - like burning cleaner fuel. Since then, a new report outlines the corporation's impact on the coastline of Europe. These vast floating cities are burning dirty bunker fuel and creating shocking levels of air pollution wherever they go. Shipping has been an unregulated industry for too long and action is urgently required.

今年 4 月，[Ecohustler 干扰了 Carnival Cruise 的股东大会](https://ecohustler.com/article/protesters-disrupt-top-corporate-polluter-carnival-corporation%27s-agm-in-london/)，呼吁股东们利用简单且经济实惠的方法（如燃烧更清洁的燃料）来减少公司污染。自那时起，一份新报告概述了该公司对欧洲海岸线的影响。这些巨大的“海上漂浮城市”燃烧肮脏的船用重油，所到之处造成令人震惊的空气污染水平。航运业长期处于无监管状态，亟需采取行动。

![](https://cdn.ecohustler.com/bWFpbi9jYXJuaXZhbC1jb3JwLWluZm9ncmFwaGljLnBuZw/carnival-corp-infographic.png?p=inline&s=1f36c1721b07a140af49a0f8637cd473&q=85&f=webp)

> Carnival Corporation, the world’s largest luxury cruise operator, emitted nearly 10 times more sulphur oxide (SOx) around European coasts than did all 260 million European cars in 2017, a [new analysis](https://google.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=0b72b745a8222140f6076e08b&id=d97c2a7644&e=09ea9d37b5) by sustainable transport group Transport & Environment reveals.

Carnival Corporation，全球最大的豪华邮轮运营商，在 2017 年环绕欧洲海岸时排放的硫氧化物（SOx）量几乎是所有 260 百万辆欧洲汽车总量的 10 倍，一项由可持续交通组织 Transport & Environment 发布的 [new analysis](https://google.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=0b72b745a8222140f6076e08b&id=d97c2a7644&e=09ea9d37b5) 证实了这一数据。

> Royal Caribbean Cruises, the world’s second largest, is second, yet four times worse than the European car fleet. SOx emissions form sulphate (SO4) aerosols that increase human health risks and contribute to acidification in terrestrial and aquatic environments.

皇家加勒比邮轮（Royal Caribbean Cruises）作为全球第二大邮轮公司排名第二，但其（SOx 排放）比整个欧洲的汽车车队还要严重四倍。

SOx 排放会形成硫酸盐（SO4）气溶胶，从而增加人类健康风险，并导致陆地和水域环境的酸化。

> In absolute terms, Spain, Italy and Greece, closely followed by France and Norway, are the European countries most exposed to SOx air pollution from cruise vessels while Barcelona, Palma de Mallorca and Venice are the most impacted European port cities, followed by Civitavecchia (Rome) and Southampton.

就绝对量而言 Spain, Italy and Greece, 紧随 France and Norway 之后，欧洲国家最易受 SOx 大气污染的游轮影响，而 Barcelona, Palma de Mallorca and Venice 是最受影响的欧洲港口城市，随后是 Civitavecchia (Rome) and Southampton。

> These countries are so exposed because they are major tourist destinations, but also because they have less stringent marine sulphur fuel standards which allows cruise ships to burn the dirtiest most sulphurous fuel all along their coastlines.

这些国家如此暴露，因为它们是主要的旅游目的地，但也因为它们有更宽松的海洋硫燃料标准，允许游轮在其海岸线上燃烧最脏、硫含量最高的燃料。

![](https://cdn.ecohustler.com/bWFpbi9jYXJuaXZhbF9hZ21fMV8uanBn/carnival_agm_1_.jpg?p=inline&s=0de7cb605af1912f8cc9812efeafbc50&q=85&f=webp)

> Faig Abbasov, shipping policy manager at T&E, said: “Luxury cruise ships are floating cities powered by some of the dirtiest fuel possible. Cities are rightly banning dirty diesel cars but they’re giving a free pass to cruise companies that spew out toxic fumes that do immeasurable harm both to those on board and on nearby shores. This is unacceptable.“

Faig Abbasov，T&E 的航运政策经理表示：“豪华邮轮是使用最肮脏燃料驱动的漂浮城市。城市正确地禁止肮脏的柴油车，却对排放有毒烟雾的邮轮公司大开方便之门；这些烟雾对船上人员和附近海岸造成了无法估量的伤害。这是不可接受的。”

> NOX emissions from cruise ships in Europe also heavily impact some cities, equivalent to about 15% of the nitrogen oxides (NOx) emitted by Europe’s passenger car fleet in a year, the report finds. In Marseille, for example, 57 cruise ships emitted in 2017 almost as much NOx as one-quarter of the city’s 340,000 passenger cars. Along the coasts of countries such as Norway, Denmark, Greece, Croatia and Malta a handful of cruise ships are also responsible for more NOx than the majority of their domestic car fleet.

报告显示，欧洲邮轮的 NOx 排放同样对部分城市造成严重影响，约相当于欧洲乘用车车队一年排放的氮氧化物（NOx）的 15%。例如，2017 年，马赛港的 57 艘邮轮排放的 NOx 几乎相当于该市 34 万辆乘用车排放量的四分之一。在挪威、丹麦、希腊、克罗地亚和马耳他等国沿海，少数几艘邮轮排放的 NOx 也超过这些国家本国乘用车车队大部分车辆的排放量。

> Europe should implement a zero-emission port standard as soon as possible, this could then be extended to other ship types. The report also recommends extending emission control areas (ECAs), currently in place only in the North and Baltic Seas and English Channel, to the rest of the European seas. Furthermore, the report recommends regulating NOx emissions from existing ships, which are currently exempt from NOx standards applying in emission control areas.

欧洲应当应尽快实施零排放港口标准，这可以随后扩展到其他船舶类型。报告还建议将排放控制区（ ECA ）扩展到欧洲其他海域。目前，这些区域仅在北海和波罗的海以及英吉利海峡生效。此外，报告还建议监管现有船舶的 NOx 排放，而这些船舶目前被豁免适用于排放控制区域的 NOx 标准。

![](https://cdn.ecohustler.com/bWFpbi9jYXJuaXZhbF8zXy5qcGc/carnival_3_.jpg?p=inline&s=23cd6755b1fc87449d3024c66db54900&q=85&f=webp)

> Faig Abbasov concluded “There are enough mature technologies to clean up cruise ships. Shore-side electricity can help cut in-port emissions, batteries are a solution for shorter distances and hydrogen technology can power even the biggest cruise ships. The cruise sector are apparently not willing to make the shift voluntarily, so we need governments to step in and mandate zero emission standards.”

Faig Abbasov 总结道：“已经有足够成熟的技术可以清洁邮轮。岸电可以帮助减少港口排放，电池是短距离航行的解决方案，而氢能技术甚至可以为最大的邮轮提供动力。邮轮行业显然不愿意主动做出转变，因此我们需要政府介入，强制推行零排放标准。”
