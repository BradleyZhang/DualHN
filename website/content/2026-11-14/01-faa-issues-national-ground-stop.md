> Much of the air traffic across the U.S. was suddenly shut down Friday after the Federal Aviation Administration ordered a nationwide ground stop for all departing flights “due to extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety.”

美国大部分航空交通周五突然中止，此前美国联邦航空管理局（Federal Aviation Administration，FAA）下令对所有离港航班实施全国范围地面停飞（ground stop），原因是“由于非同寻常的情况和出于安全考虑”。

> The unexpected and highly unusual alert, which was issued at 3:54 p.m. ET, contained no other information beyond stating that “all departing traffic should remain ground stopped until further notice.”

意外且极不寻常的警报于 3:54 p.m. ET 发布，除陈述“所有离境航班应保持地面停止，直至另行通知”外，并未包含其他信息。

> “Inbound international flights will be managed on a case by case basis,” the FAA said.

国际进境航班将按照个案处理， FAA 表示。

> When the FAA issued the alert, 4,042 flights headed for U.S. destinations were airborne, [according to Flightradar24](https://x.com/flightradar24/status/2121379617252810820?a=roPvFTWwSk7-tU2h8k0&s=20), a Swedish service that displays real-time aircraft flight tracking information.

当 FAA 发出警报时，4,042  个航班正在飞往 U.S. 目的地，[据 Flightradar24] (https://x.com/flightradar24/status/2121379617252810820?a=roPvFTWwSk7-tU2h8k0&s=20)，一个瑞典服务，显示实时航空器航班跟踪信息。

> Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said there was “no indication as of yet” that there was anything “nefarious” that caused the ground stop. He said it was the result of a telecommunications outage “as of now.”

Homeland Security 部长 Markwayne Mullin said “截至目前”没有任何“恶意的”因素导致地面停飞。他表示，这目前是通信中断导致的。

> Mullin said President Donald Trump has been briefedand that they “are not ruling out anything at this point” in terms of what caused the communications issue. He said those currently on flights have no reason for concern. “We are assured that everybody is safe,” he said.

马林表示，总统唐纳德·特朗普已听取简报，就通信问题的成因而言，他们“目前不排除任何可能性”。他说，目前在航班上的人没有理由担心。“我们确信所有人都安全，”他说。

> While the investigation into the failure is in a very early stage, there is no indication that the incident has been caused by malicious activity, officials from multiple agencies that are involved in the situation told NBC News.

虽然针对该故障的调查仍处于非常早期阶段，但参与此事的多个机构的官员告诉 NBC News，目前没有迹象表明该事件是由恶意活动引起的。

> They said they haven’t seen the type of activity that would normally be associated with a cyberattack. The officials, however, cautioned that the assessment could change as investigators learn more.

他们表示，尚未看到通常与网络攻击相关的活动。然而，官员警告称，随着调查人员了解更多信息，此评估可能会发生变化。

> Three senior officials said there’s nothing that indicates a connection between Friday’s incident with the FAA’s systems and the Microsoft outage the day before.

三名高级官员表示，没有什么迹象表明周五 FAA 系统发生的事件与前一天 Microsoft 发生的故障之间存在联系。

> Several hours after the ground stop was announced, a top aviation expert estimated that anywhere from 300,000 to 400,000 passengers were stranded — and it was anybody’s guess when they would get to their destinations.

地面停飞令宣布数小时后，一位顶尖航空专家估计，有多达 30 万至 40 万名乘客滞留——至于他们何时才能抵达目的地，谁都说不准。

> “Daily we have about 25,000 flights in U.S. domestic markets,” Vikrant Vaze, a Dartmouth professor who specializes in commercial aviation logistics, wrote in an email to NBC News. “I would say we should have around 3,000 to 4,000 flights held up at this time over the past 2 plus hours.”

“我们每天在美国国内市场大约有 25,000 个航班，”专攻商业航空物流的达特茅斯学院教授 Vikrant Vaze 在给 NBC News 的电子邮件中写道，“我想说，在过去 2 个多小时里，此时应该有大约 3,000 到 4,000 个航班被延误。”

> But by 6 p.m., Reagan Washington National Airport in Arlington, Virginia, was at capacity, and Chicago O’Hare International Airport was nearing capacity, meaning there was no place for the planes to park, a source with knowledge told NBC News.



到傍晚6点，弗吉尼亚州阿灵顿的里根华盛顿国家机场已达到容量上限，芝加哥奥黑尔国际机场也接近饱和，这意味着飞机无处停放，知情人士向 NBC 新闻透露。

> With planes stranded on runways, the likelihood increased that some of these planes would have to be diverted to other airports, the source said.

飞机停在跑道上后，某些飞机转发至其他机场的可能性提高了，来源称。

> Meanwhile, Air Space Intelligence, the AI tech startup that is a vendor for the FAA on its new [SMART system](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/live-blog/live-updates-flights-grounded-faa-communications-outage-rcna609268/rcrd123876?canonicalCard=true) for air traffic control, insisted its software was not responsible for the nationwide ground stop.



与此同时，作为美国联邦航空局 (FAA) 新型空中交通管制 [SMART系统](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/live-blog/live-updates-flights-grounded-faa-communications-outage-rcna609268/rcrd123876?canonicalCard=true) 供应商的 AI 科技初创公司 Air Space Intelligence 坚称，其软件并非导致全国性地面停飞的原因。

> “The current ground stop is not based on or a result of the SMART system, any product of ASI or our integration with FAA and ATC,” the company said in a statement [posted to X](https://x.com/AirSpaceIntel/status/2121371183836696583).

“当前的地面停飞并非基于或由 SMART 系统、ASI 的任何产品或我们与 FAA 和 ATC 的集成所导致，”该公司在一份[发布到 X](https://x.com/AirSpaceIntel/status/2121371183836696583)的声明中表示。

> Aviation safety expert Jeff Guzzetti called the outage “extraordinary” and said he doesn’t know what would have caused it.

航空安全专家 Jeff Guzzetti 将此次中断称为“ extraordinary”，并表示他不清楚是什么导致了它。

> Guzzetti emphasized that pilots are trained to operate through communications outages and that there are redundancies in place for such instances. Pilots “could get information relayed through other means” to ensure planes are spaced properly for landing.

Guzzetti 强调，飞行员受过训练，能够在通信中断情况下运行，并有相应的冗余系统应对此类情况。飞行员可以通过其他途径获取信息，以确保飞机按间距安全着陆。

> He added that the situation is turning from a safety issue to a massive logistics problem. Airports are becoming crowded with planes trying to land as the aircraft currently on the ground aren’t able to depart. Planes on the ground may have to move and park on taxiways, Guzzetti said.

他补充说，事态正从安全问题转变为大规模的物流难题。由于地面滞留的飞机无法起飞，试图降落的飞机导致机场变得拥挤不堪。古泽蒂表示，地面上的飞机可能不得不移动并停在滑行道上。

> There are different layers of communications — high-altitude, midlevel and the towers on the ground. The high-altitude control centers are the ones affected by the outage.

通信分为不同层级——高空层、中层和地面塔楼。受中断影响的是高空控制中心。

> Shortly before the FAA issued its alert, both major airports in Chicago — O’Hare and Midway International Airport — were listed on a ground stop alert due to an “equipment outage,” [NBC Chicago](https://www.nbcchicago.com/news/local/faa-issues-nationwide-ground-stop-due-to-extraordinary-circumstances-and-safety/4016516/) reported.

就在 FAA 发布警报前不久，芝加哥的两大主要机场——O’Hare 和 Midway International Airport——因“设备故障”被列入地面停飞警报，[NBC Chicago](https://www.nbcchicago.com/news/local/faa-issues-nationwide-ground-stop-due-to-extraordinary-circumstances-and-safety/4016516/) 报道。

> [Deveren Werne](https://www.linkedin.com/in/deverenwerne/) was on an American Airlines jet that was about to take off from Indianapolis to Chicago when suddenly the pilots announced that all flights were grounded.

[Deveren Werne](https://www.linkedin.com/in/deverenwerne/) 坐在一架即将起飞的 American Airlines 飞机上，从 Indianapolis 飞往 Chicago 时，飞行员突然宣布所有航班已取消。

> “At first, the pilot announced that there was some kind of radio issue,” Werne, 55, of Greenville, South Carolina, told NBC News in a telephone interview. “Then a little while later, the pilot said something like, ‘There is something going on that’s more serious than that.’”

“一开始，机长宣布出现了无线电故障，”55岁的韦恩（来自南卡罗来纳州格林威尔）在接受NBC新闻电话采访时说。“过了一会儿，机长又说了一句，大概是：‘情况比这更严重了。’”

> Werne, a consultant who helps manufacturers and other industries adapt AI technology, said he is a frequent flyer and this was a first for him. He said there was “a mixture of emotions” from his fellow passengers on hearing the announcement.

Werne，一位帮助制造商和其他行业引入 AI 技术的顾问，他说自己是常飞客，但这次是头一遭。他表示，听到公告后，同机乘客中出现了“情感交织”的情况。

> “Then I started hearing from other friends who were on planes, including one in Chicago who was stuck on a runway,” Werne said. “Right now, our plane is heading back to the terminal, so at least we’re going to be able to deplane.”

“然后我开始听到其他在飞机上的朋友的消息，其中有一位在芝加哥的朋友被困在跑道上，” Werne 说。“现在我们乘坐的飞机正返回航站楼，这样我们至少能下飞机了。”

United Airlines 发布声明，确认了 ground stop。声明表示：“已经飞起的 United 航班将继续飞行。” “在 FAA ground stop 持续生效期间，新的国内航班将不得起飞。”

> Nationwide ground stops are extremely rare.

全国范围的地面停运极为罕见。

> After the Sept. 11, 2001, terrorist attacks, the FAA ordered an unprecedented nationwide ground stop. All civilian air traffic across U.S. airspace was halted and airborne planes were ordered to land at the nearest available airports.

在 2001年9月11日 发生的恐怖袭击后，FAA 下令实施 前所未有的 全国范围地面停飞。所有在 U.S. 空域 内的民用航空交通均被中断，空中航班被命令降落在最近可用的机场。

> In January 2023, the [FAA grounded all flights for a few hours,](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/faa-notam-system-crashed-target-reforms-rcna65272) blaming a failure in the Notice to Air Missions system, which alerts pilots to a host of potential dangers.

2023 年 1 月，[FAA 停飞所有航班数小时，](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/faa-notam-system-crashed-target-reforms-rcna65272) 将其归咎于 Notice to Air Missions 系统的故障，该系统用于提醒飞行员注意各种潜在危险。
