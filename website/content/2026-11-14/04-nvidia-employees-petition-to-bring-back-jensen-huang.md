> Some Nvidia employees are calling for [Jensen Huang’s](https://www.wired.com/tag/jensen-huang) return even after his multiple reported extramarital affairs and [abrupt resignation](https://www.wired.com/story/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-steps-down/), according to internal correspondence reviewed by WIRED.

一些 Nvidia 员工呼吁[Jensen Huang’s](https://www.wired.com/tag/jensen-huang)回归即使他的多次报导的婚外情和[突如其来的辞职](https://www.wired.com/story/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-steps-down/)，据 WIRED 审阅的内部通信。

> An internal “suggestion box” post submitted before Huang’s resignation by a longtime engineer at [Nvidia](https://www.wired.com/tag/nvidia/) urged the company’s board to take employee sentiment into consideration prior to taking any action. The submission has attracted more than 1,600 comments since then, with several asking board members to find a way to bring back Huang.

在 Huang 辞职之前，Nvidia 的一位资深工程师在公司内部"意见箱"上发布了一篇帖子，敦促公司董事会在采取任何行动之前考虑员工的感受。该帖子自发布以来已吸引超过 1,600 条评论，其中多条评论呼吁董事会成员想办法让 Huang 回归。

> “If you believe in the mission, bring back THE boss,” wrote one anonymous commenter.



一位匿名评论者写道：“如果你相信这个使命，就把老板带回来。”

> “We are all humans and can make mistakes. Jensen did so many good things and he needs a second chance,” wrote another.

“我们都是人类，会犯错。Jensen 做了很多好事，他需要第二次机会，”另一人写道。

> One employee went as far as sending a companywide email demanding transparency into Huang’s exit. “I stand by Jensen,” the staffer wrote. “Not because I believe any person is infallible, but because I refuse to believe that standing for fairness must mean standing at a distance from someone whose leadership has meant so much to us.”

一位员工甚至更进一步，向全公司发送了一封邮件，要求透明公开 Huang 离职的内情。“我支持 Jensen，”这位员工写道，“并不是因为我相信任何人都是完美无缺的，而是因为我拒绝承认，支持公平就意味着必须与一位其领导对我们意义如此重大的人保持距离。”

> The employee couldn’t be reached for comment, while the engineer behind the suggestion box post declined to comment.

无法联系到该员工置评，而意见箱帖文背后的工程师拒绝置评。

> Nvidia and its new board chair, investor Harvey Jones, didn’t immediately respond to requests for comment.

Nvidia 及其新任董事会主席、投资人 Harvey Jones 没有立即回应置评请求。

> Huang, a 63-year-old who had led Nvidia since its founding in 1993, wrote in an email to employees on Sunday that he had been “unfaithful to \[his\] wife” and that there was “no excuse.” The acknowledgement followed reports of an affair with a [suspected Chinese intelligence asset](https://www.wired.com/story/chinas-nvidia-honeypot-isnt-surprising-jensen-huang-fell-for-it-anyway/). The board announced Huang’s resignation on Thursday morning, following [reports](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/11/technology/jensen-huang-nvidia-affair.html) of an alleged second affair with a direct report. (This employee has not commented on the matter.) Since then, Reuters [reported](https://www.reuters.com/technology/former-nvidia-ceo-huang-had-relationship-with-second-employee-sources-say-2026-11-12/) on an alleged affair with yet another Nvidia employee. Huang hasn’t commented about these reports since his Sunday email.

黄仁勋（Huang），一位自 1993 年 Nvidia 成立以来一直领导该公司的 63 岁人士，周日在给员工的电子邮件中写道，他“对[其]妻子不忠”，并且“没有借口”。

这一承认是在有关他与一名[涉嫌中国情报资产](https://www.wired.com/story/chinas-nvidia-honeypot-isnt-surprising-jensen-huang-fell-for-it-anyway/)的报道之后发生的。

董事会于周四早晨宣布了 Huang 的辞职，此前有[报道](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/11/technology/jensen-huang-nvidia-affair.html)称他与一名直属下属涉嫌发生第二段婚外情。（该员工对此事未发表评论。）

此后，Reuters [报道](https://www.reuters.com/technology/former-nvidia-ceo-huang-had-relationship-with-second-employee-sources-say-2026-11-12/)称他与另一名 Nvidia 员工涉嫌有婚外情。

自周日发出电子邮件以来，Huang 就这些报道未发表评论。

> Nvidia’s board is largely made up of early Nvidia investors and Huang’s longtime confidants. It named Nvidia chief financial officer [Colette Kress](https://www.wired.com/story/jensen-huang-colette-kress-replacement-powerful-women-in-tech/) [interim CEO](https://www.wired.com/story/who-is-colette-kress-the-woman-who-could-succeed-jensen-huang/) and appointed former Synopsys CEO Harvey Jones as chair of the board. The crisis has cost the company [$1 trillion in market value](https://www.wired.com/story/jensen-huang-tanked-a-trillion-dollars-in-less-than-a-month/) since the allegations surfaced, prompted investigations from US lawmakers and the FBI, and raised the prospect of new export controls on chips.

Nvidia 的董事会主要由早期 Nvidia 投资者和黄仁勋的长期亲信组成。该公司任命 Nvidia 首席财务官 [Colette Kress](https://www.wired.com/story/jensen-huang-colette-kress-replacement-powerful-women-in-tech/) 为临时 [CEO](https://www.wired.com/story/who-is-colette-kress-the-woman-who-could-succeed-jensen-huang/)，并任命前 Synopsys CEO Harvey Jones 为董事会主席。自指控曝光以来，这场危机已使该公司损失了 [1 万亿美元的市值](https://www.wired.com/story/jensen-huang-tanked-a-trillion-dollars-in-less-than-a-month/)，引发了美国立法者和 FBI 的调查，并可能引发新的芯片出口管制。

> It’s been business as usual inside the company throughout this week, but [Huang’s resignation left many employees stunned](https://www.wired.com/story/silicon-valley-is-still-defending-jensen-huang/). In the hours after, about two dozen employees posted on LinkedIn in support of Huang, with one describing their faith in his leadership as unwavering. Over the same period, WIRED couldn’t identify any posts from Nvidia employees in support of his resignation.

本周内，公司内部运作一如既往，但 [Huang 辞职让许多员工震惊](https://www.wired.com/story/silicon-valley-is-still-defending-jensen-huang/)。事发后几小时内，约二十四名员工在 LinkedIn 上发帖声援 Huang，其中一人表示对其领导力的信心始终如一。同期，WIRED 未能找到任何 Nvidia 员工发帖支持其辞职的记录。

> One employee who has been at Nvidia for a couple of years and wasn’t authorized to speak publicly says they perceive the desire for Huang’s reinstatement as widespread. They worry the company will eventually suffer without its founder at the helm and say that the board must bring him back. “Nvidia is Nvidia because of Jensen,” the employee tells WIRED.

一位在英伟达工作了几年、未获授权公开发言的雇员表示，他们认为希望黄仁勋回归的呼声十分普遍。他们担心，公司若长期缺少这位创始人掌舵，最终将遭受损失，并称董事会必须把他请回来。“英伟达之所以是英伟达，就是因为有黄仁勋，”这位雇员对 WIRED 表示。

> Many employees had expected a board investigation to conclude before any big changes would occur. For instance, the suggestion-box post called on the board to share its findings with employees and formally solicit their sentiment “about Jensen’s continuing as our CEO.”

许多员工原本曾预计，董事会调查会在任何大变化发生之前结束。例如，一条建议箱帖子的呼吁，要求董事会分享其调查结果，并正式征求员工对“ Jensen 继续担任我们的 CEO” 的看法。

> Instead, Huang left just as the inquiry had begun. Staffers are seeking to better understand why. “If America fails the man who helped build its future,” the employee in the companywide email stated, “then this world does not deserve Jensen Huang.”

相反，黄简森就在调查刚刚开始时离开了。工作人员正努力更好地理解原因。“If America fails the man who helped build its future, the employee in the companywide email stated, ‘Then this world does not deserve Jensen Huang.’”
