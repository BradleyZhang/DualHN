> #### Amazon’s Decoy Deals: Groundwork Investigation Reveals Nearly 1 in 3 Black Friday and Cyber Monday “Deals” Advertised by Amazon Offered No Savings to Shoppers, Nearly 1 in 10 Were Actually More Expensive

#### Amazon 的诱饵式促销：Groundwork 调查揭示，Amazon 在 Black Friday 和 Cyber Monday 宣传的近 1/3“优惠”并未给购物者带来任何节省，近 1/10 实际上更贵

> A blockbuster new investigation from [Groundwork Collaborative](https://groundworkcollaborative.org/) exposes how **Amazon shoppers may be getting hosed during the retail giant’s much-touted Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale events**. The report, *[Deal or Decoy? Amazon’s Deceptive Discounts](http://groundworkcollaborative.org/amazon)*, examines products the retailer showcased as some the “best” discounts of the year to shoppers hunting for bargains during these sale events. A closer look at hundreds of these “deals” finds that Amazon frequently offered shoppers the same price, or in some cases a higher price, during recent holiday sale events than they could have paid just weeks earlier.

Groundwork Collaborative 发布的一项重磅新调查揭露，亚马逊购物者在该零售巨头大肆宣传的黑色星期五（Black Friday）和网络星期一（Cyber Monday）大促期间可能正在吃亏。该报告《优惠还是诱饵？亚马逊的欺骗性折扣》审视了该零售商在促销活动中向寻觅优惠的购物者展示的部分“最佳”折扣产品。对数百个此类“优惠”的深入分析发现，亚马逊在近期假日大促期间向购物者提供的价格，往往与几周前持平，甚至在某些情况下价格更高。

> **The report’s authors, Breyon Williams, Elizabeth Pancotti, and Lindsay Owens, find that nearly 1 in 3 of these Amazon “deals” offered no real savings** – the exact same products were sold for the same price or less in the month before the sale. **During Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales in the past two years, prices for nearly 1 in 10 products advertised as “deals” were *higher*** than what customers could have paid for the products in the month leading up to the sales.



**该报告的作者 Breyon Williams、Elizabeth Pancotti 和 Lindsay Owens 发现，在这些 Amazon “deals” 中，近三分之一并没有提供真正的省钱优惠**——在促销前的一个月里，完全相同的产品售价相同或更低。**在过去两年的 Black Friday 和 Cyber Monday 促销期间，近十分之一标榜为 “deals” 的产品价格甚至*更高***，高于促销前一个月顾客本可以支付的产品价格。

> Groundwork’s analysis examined the prices of more than 350 products included on Amazon’s own deal pages for the Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale events in 2024 and 2025. These pages promote these deals as some of the “best” deals available. Amazon’s Black Friday 2025 deals page says, “With so many deals to explore, we’ve handpicked some of the best ones you’ll find.”



Groundwork 的分析研究了 2024 年和 2025 年黑色星期五（Black Friday）与网络星期一（Cyber Monday）促销活动中，Amazon 自身优惠页面收录的 350 多种产品的价格。这些页面将这些优惠宣传为现有的“最佳”优惠。Amazon 2025 年黑色星期五优惠页面写道：“有这么多优惠可以探索，我们精心挑选了一些你能找到的最好的优惠。”

> **Groundwork Collaborative President and CEO and report co-author [Lindsay Owens](https://groundworkcollaborative.org/person/lindsay-owens/), shared the following reaction:**

**Groundwork Collaborative 主席兼首席执行官及报告合著者 [Lindsay Owens](https://groundworkcollaborative.org/person/lindsay-owens/)，发表了如下反应：**

> “Millions of Americans wait all year for Black Friday and Cyber Monday to score the biggest bargains on stocking stuffers and household staples. Shoppers trust that the deals advertised to them deliver real savings because Amazon promotes them that way. But it turns out that these major sales aren’t all they’re cracked up to be. Instead, our investigation reveals that shoppers who wait for these major sale events may be playing rip-off roulette.

数百万美国人全年都在等待 Black Friday 和 Cyber Monday，以便在圣诞袜小礼物和家庭日用品上抢到最划算的优惠。购物者相信，向他们宣传的这些交易能带来真正的节省，因为 Amazon 就是这样宣传的。但事实证明，这些大型促销并不像宣传的那么好。相反，我们的调查揭示，等待这些大型促销活动的购物者可能是在玩一场“宰客轮盘赌”。

> “Amazon dominates online shopping, and families count on its sale events to stretch their dollars. With the holiday shopping season upon us and prices continuing to rise, shoppers deserve to know they can trust every deal Amazon promises them. If Amazon can’t deliver that basic promise, lawmakers should hold them accountable.”

亚马逊主导了在线购物，家庭们依靠其促销活动来节省开支。随着假日购物季来临，物价持续上涨，消费者理应知道他们可以信任亚马逊许下的每一笔交易。如果亚马逊无法兑现这一基本承诺，立法者就应当追究其责任。

> Owens is the author of the recent book *[Gouged: The End of a Fair Price—And What That Means for Your Wallet](https://gougedbook.com/)*, which exposes how corporations are sophisticated pricing schemes to overcharge consumers.

欧文斯是近期新书 *《Gouged: The End of a Fair Price—And What That Means for Your Wallet》* 的作者，该书揭露了企业如何利用复杂的定价手段向消费者收取过高费用。

> - **The report’s key findings include:**
> 	- **The price of nearly 1 in 3 products that Amazon described as “deals” was the same – or higher – during sale events than what customers could have purchased them for in the month leading up to the sale.**
> 		- **Some Amazon “deals” offered shoppers higher prices than they could have paid before the sale.**
> 		- **On Black Friday 2025,** a pair of yellow Hunter Women’s Short Rain Boots “on sale” for $126.99 had been $79.97 nine days before the sale event started, meaning customers looking for a deal could’ve paid 59% more if they waited for the sale.
> 				- **On Cyber Monday 2025,** a pair of men’s UGG Butte boots “on sale” for $179.98 had been $98.40 roughly four weeks before the start of the sale event, meaning the “deal” cost 83% more than the recent low.
> 				- **Among these “worse” deals, the median sale-event price was 19% higher than what shoppers could have paid for the products in the weeks leading up to the sale.**
> - **Since learning of our study, Amazon has removed the aforementioned deal pages our study drew from,** but archived versions of these pages can be found at the following links: [Black Friday 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20241126224714/https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/best-black-friday-deals-amazon), [Cyber Monday 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20241213115036/https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/cyber-monday-deals-2024-amazon), [Black Friday 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20251213195446/https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-black-friday-best-deals-shop-2025-us), [Cyber Monday 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20251208033637/https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/cyber-monday-tech-deals-2025-us).
> - **Amidst the ongoing affordability crisis, millions of Americans count on holiday sale events, like Black Friday, to deliver real savings.** In 2025, a [record](https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/thanksgiving-holiday-weekend-draws-a-record-203-million-shoppers) 203 million Americans shopped between Thanksgiving and Cyber Monday, and this year, [70%](https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/2026/black-friday-through-cyber-monday-weekend-sales-to-surpass-$85-billion-bain-company/) of U.S. customers plan to take advantage of holiday promotions.
> - ***Deal or Decoy* calls on federal and state regulators to crack down on misleading discounts** and require retailers to show the lowest price a product sold for in the previous 30 days when advertising a sale.

- **报告的主要发现包括：**
	- **在 Amazon 标注为"优惠"的商品中，近三分之一在促销活动期间的售价与消费者在大促前一个月内购买所需支付的价格相同，甚至更高。**
		- **一些 Amazon 的"优惠"向消费者提供的价格甚至高于大促前的价格。**
		- **在 2025 年 Black Friday，** 一双黄色 Hunter Women's Short Rain Boots 的"促销价"为 $126.99，而在大促开始前九天，其价格仅为 $79.97，这意味着等待促销的消费者可能多支付 59% 的费用。
				- **在 2025 年 Cyber Monday，** 一双男士 UGG Butte 靴子的"促销价"为 $179.98，而在大促开始前约四周，其价格仅为 $98.40，这意味着该"优惠"比近期最低价高出 83%。
				- **在这些"更差"的优惠中，促销期间的中位价格比消费者在大促前几周购买所需支付的价格高出 19%。**
- **在得知我们的研究后，Amazon 已删除了我们研究引用的上述优惠页面，但这些页面的存档版本可通过以下链接访问：** [Black Friday 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20241126224714/https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/best-black-friday-deals-amazon), [Cyber Monday 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20241213115036/https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/cyber-monday-deals-2024-amazon), [Black Friday 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20251213195446/https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-black-friday-best-deals-shop-2025-us), [Cyber Monday 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20251208033637/https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/cyber-monday-tech-deals-2025-us).
- **在持续的生活成本危机中，数百万美国人都指望 Black Friday 等假日促销活动带来真正的省钱机会。** 2025 年，在 Thanksgiving 至 Cyber Monday 期间，共有创纪录的 2.03 亿美国消费者进行了购物，而今年，[70%](https://www.bain.com/about/media-center/press-releases/2026/black-friday-through-cyber-monday-weekend-sales-to-surpass-$85-billion-bain-company/) 的美国消费者计划利用假日促销活动。
- ***Deal or Decoy*** 呼吁联邦和州监管机构打击误导性折扣行为，并要求零售商在宣传促销时展示该商品过去 30 天内的最低售价。

> **ADDITIONAL BACKGROUND**

**补充背景**

> **The new report builds on Groundwork’s work exposing corporate pricing abuses and delivering results for American consumers.**

新的报告基于 Groundwork 的工作，该工作揭露了企业定价滥用行为，并为美国消费者带来了成果。

> - In December 2025, Groundwork teamed up with Consumer Reports and More Perfect Union to [expose how Instacart](https://groundworkcollaborative.org/work/instacart/) was charging shoppers different prices for the exact same groceries at the same store and time.
> 	- The findings made [national headlines](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/12/09/business/instacart-algorithmic-pricing.html), triggered investigations by the [Federal Trade Commission](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/ftc-investigating-instacarts-ai-pricing-tool-source-says-2025-12-17/) and [New York Attorney General](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-demands-answers-instacart-about-algorithmic-pricing), and prompted Instacart to [end the practice](https://company.instacart.com/updates/ending-item-price-tests-on-instacart) just weeks later.
> - Following the publication of Groundwork’s Owens’ book *Gouged: The End of a Fair Price—And What That Means for Your Wallet*, the Federal Trade Commission launched an [investigation](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/10/ftc-launches-personalized-pricing-compensation-inquiry) into major retailers’ use of customers’ personal data to set prices, adding to growing scrutiny of corporate pricing practices and their impact on Americans’ wallets in response to Groundwork and Owens’ research.
> - In October 2026, Groundwork exposed Walmart’s creepy patent filings and pricing playbook. The [report](https://groundworkcollaborative.org/work/the-walmart-watchtower/) digs into patent filings, privacy disclosures, and technology investments to reveal how the retailer is building the infrastructure that could allow it to deploy personalized pricing at scale.

- 2025 年 12 月，Groundwork 联合 Consumer Reports 与 More Perfect Union，[揭露了 Instacart](https://groundworkcollaborative.org/work/instacart/) 在同一时间、同一商店对同一杂货向不同购物者收取不同价格的做法。
	- 该调查结果登上[全国头条](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/12/09/business/instacart-algorithmic-pricing.html)，引发[联邦贸易委员会 (FTC)](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/ftc-investigating-instacarts-ai-pricing-tool-source-says-2025-12-17/) 和[纽约州总检察长](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-demands-answers-instacart-about-algorithmic-pricing)的调查，并迫使 Instacart 在几周后[终止了该做法](https://company.instacart.com/updates/ending-item-price-tests-on-instacart)。
- 随着 Groundwork 研究员 Owens 的新书 *Gouged: The End of a Fair Price—And What That Means for Your Wallet* 出版，联邦贸易委员会发起[调查](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/10/ftc-launches-personalized-pricing-compensation-inquiry)，针对大型零售商利用客户个人数据制定价格的行为，这进一步加剧了在 Groundwork 和 Owens 研究推动下，对企业定价行为及其对美国民众钱包影响的审视。
- 2026 年 10 月，Groundwork 曝光了 Walmart 令人不安的专利申请与定价策略手册。该[报告](https://groundworkcollaborative.org/work/the-walmart-watchtower/)深入挖掘专利文件、隐私披露及技术投资，揭示这家零售商正在构建大规模部署个性化定价所需的基础设施。
