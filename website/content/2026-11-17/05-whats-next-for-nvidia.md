> ## What’s Next For Nvidia?

## Nvidia 接下来的规划？

> Nvidia’s board [announced](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-CEO-Transition/) that Jensen Huang had resigned 33 years after he co-founded the company.

Nvidia 的董事会 [宣布](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-CEO-Transition/) 表示，Jensen Huang 在联合创立该公司 33 年后辞职。

> What happens to a company when a visionary CEO is gone? Most often innovation dies and the company coasts for years on momentum and its brand. Rarely does it regain its former glory.

当一位富有远见的CEO离职时，公司通常会失去创新动力，只能靠着原有的势头和品牌维持多年，很难再恢复到从前的辉煌。

> That’s the danger facing Nvidia. Here’s what their board should be considering.

这就是英伟达面临的危险。以下是其董事会应当考量的事项。

> Jensen Huang reinvented Nvidia three times. In 2006 he launched [CUDA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CUDA), turning a graphics chip for PC gamers into a general-purpose computing platform – and funded it for years ahead of any revenue. In 2012, when researchers used Nvidia chips to win the [ImageNet contest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ImageNet), he pointed the company to deep learning. In 2020, with the [acquisition of Mellanox](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-completes-acquisition-of-mellanox-creating-major-force-driving-next-gen-data-centers), Nvidia went from selling boards with GPUs to selling integrated racks that power AI datacenters.

Jensen Huang 三次重塑了 Nvidia。2006年，他推出了 [CUDA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CUDA)，将面向 PC 玩家的显卡转变为通用计算平台——并提前多年为其提供资金，早于获得收入。2012年，当研究人员使用 Nvidia 芯片赢得 [ImageNet 竞赛](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ImageNet) 时，他将公司引向深度学习。2020年，随着 [Mellanox 的收购](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-completes-acquisition-of-mellanox-creating-major-force-driving-next-gen-data-centers)，Nvidia 从销售配备 GPU 的板卡，转向销售驱动 AI 数据中心的集成机架。

> These were not just product transitions, but radical business model transitions; new channels, new customers and new markets. Nvidia pivoted from a vendor of graphics boards, to a software platform, and then to systems company. It’s hard to imagine a CEO focused on executing an existing business model funding [CUDA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/on-demand/session/gtc25-S72571/) through those years.

这些不仅仅是产品转型，而是彻底的业务模式转型； 新渠道、新客户和新市场。 Nvidia 从图形板供应商转型为软件平台，随后转型为系统公司。 想象一下， 一个专注于执行现有业务模式并为 [CUDA] (https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/on-demand/session/gtc25-S72571/) 通过这些年 CEO 很难想象。

![](https://i0.wp.com/steveblank.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/nvidia-reinventions.jpg?w=1430&ssl=1)

> Steve Jobs did the same at Apple, transforming it from a niche computer company into the most profitable company in the world. Between 2001 and 2008, Jobs reinvented the company three times.

史蒂夫·乔布斯在苹果公司做了同样的事情，将一家小众电脑公司转变为全球最有利可图的公司。2001年至2008年期间，乔布斯三次对这家公司进行了彻底革新。

> Visionary CEOs are not “just” great at assuring world-class *execution* of a successful business model, they are also world-class *innovators*.

有远见的 CEO 并不是仅仅擅长确保一个成功的商业模式的世界级执行，他们也是世界级的创新者。

> The best are agile and know how to pivot, making substantive changes to their business model while or before their market has shifted. The very best of them *shape* markets – they know how to create new markets by seeing opportunities *before anyone else*. They remain entrepreneurs.

最优秀的创业者灵活敏捷，懂得如何转型，能在市场发生转变之前或同时，对其商业模式做出实质性的调整。其中最顶尖的创业者能够*塑造*市场——他们懂得通过*比任何人都更早*地发现机会来创造新市场。他们始终保持着创业者的本色。

![](https://i0.wp.com/steveblank.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/sweet-spot-for-transformation.jpg?w=300&ssl=1)

> Visionary CEOs don’t need someone else to demo the company’s key products for them. They deeply understand products, and they have their own coherent and consistent vision of where the industry/business models and customers are today, and where they need to take the company. They know who their customers are because they spend time talking to them. They use strategy committees and the exec staff for advice, but *none of these CEOs pivot by committee*.

有远见的 CEO 不需要别人为他们演示公司的核心产品。他们深刻理解产品，对行业/商业模式和客户的现状，以及公司需要走向何方，有着自己连贯一致的愿景。他们了解自己的客户，因为他们会花时间与客户交流。他们会借助战略委员会和高管团队来获取建议，但*这些 CEO 从不会通过委员会来做出转型决策*。

> Microsoft and Apple are two examples of what happens when a visionary CEO is replaced by an execution CEO.

微软和苹果是当一名前锋CEO被替换为执行CEO时，会发生何种结局的两个典型例子。

> **Microsoft and the Cost of Missing the Next Market  
> **Microsoft entered the 21st century as the dominant software provider for anyone who interacted with a computing device. After running Microsoft for 25 years, Bill Gates handed the reins of Microsoft to Steve Ballmer in January 2000. Ballmer went on to run Microsoft for the next 14 years.

**微软与错失下一个市场的代价**

进入 21 世纪时，对于任何与计算设备交互的人来说，微软都是占主导地位的软件提供商。在执掌微软 25 年后，比尔·盖茨于 2000 年 1 月将微软的领导权交给了史蒂夫·鲍尔默。鲍尔默随后继续执掌微软 14 年。

> Ballmer organized the company around *execution* of its then current strengths – Windows and Office businesses. He tripled Microsoft’s sales to $78 billion and profits more than doubled from $9 billion to $22 billion, but those results hid missed opportunities that would reshape computing.

巴勒默围绕当时公司的核心优势——Windows和Office业务——进行了组织架构调整。他将微软的销售额提高了三倍，达到780亿美元，利润则从90亿美元增长到220多亿美元，但这些成就掩盖了错失一些本可以重塑计算机行业的机会。

> Ballmer failed to understand and execute on the five most important technology trends of the 21st century: in search – losing to Google; in smartphones – losing to Apple; in mobile operating systems – losing to Google/Apple; in media – losing to Apple/Netflix; and in the cloud – losing to Amazon.

Ballmer 未能理解并执行 21 世纪五大最重要的技术趋势： 在搜索引擎领域，输给 Google；在智能手机领域，输给 Apple；在移动操作系统领域，输给 Google/Apple；在媒体领域，输给 Apple/Netflix；以及在云端，输给 Amazon。

> In early 2014, Satya Nadella took charge. Nadella got Microsoft organized around the cloud (Azure), freed the Office and Azure teams from Windows and killed the phone business. Nadella saved Microsoft from irrelevance. Today Microsoft is worth [about $4 trillion](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/11/microsoft-tops-nvidia039s-market-cap-for-first-time-since-june-2025). It is one of the few companies that regained its former glory, after its board replaced a salesman with a product and engineering insider.



2014 年初，萨提亚·纳德拉（Satya Nadella）执掌微软。纳德拉带领微软围绕云（Azure）进行重组，将 Office 和 Azure 团队从 Windows 的束缚中解放出来，并砍掉了手机业务。纳德拉拯救了微软，使其免于走向边缘化。如今，微软的市值已达到 [约 $4 万亿美元](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/11/microsoft-tops-nvidia039s-market-cap-for-first-time-since-june 2025)。在其董事会用一位产品和工程背景的内部人士取代了销售型高管之后，微软成为了少数重振昔日辉煌的公司之一。

> **Innovation CEOs Hire Execution VPs  
> ***Successful* visionary CEOs build an executive staff of world-class operating executives and technical leaders (and they unconsciously force out any world-class innovators from their direct reports). The problem is in a company driven by a visionary CEO; there is only one visionary.

**创新CEO聘请执行副总裁  
***成功的* 愿景型CEO构建了一个由世界级运营高管和技术领袖组成的高管团队（并且他们潜意识地将任何世界级创新者从其直接下属中排挤出去）。问题在于，在一个由愿景型CEO驱动的公司中，只有一个有远见的人。

![](https://i0.wp.com/steveblank.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/visionary-ceo-org-chart.jpg?w=468&ssl=1)

> Nvidia is the extreme case. Jensen had [about 60 direct reports](https://fortune.com/2024/11/12/jensen-huang-nvidia-ceo-leadership-mpp) and discouraged one-on-one meetings. No long-term plans. Much like Jobs, he made himself the point where technical, product and business decisions came together. Replacing that role is harder than filling a box on an organization chart. He said he [didn’t believe in succession planning](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-12/jensen-huang-nvidia-ceo-succession-plan-after-spy-plot/107376722).

Nvidia 是一个极端案例。Jensen 有大约 60 名直接下属，并且不鼓励一对一会议。没有长期计划。与 Jobs 非常相似，他将自己打造成技术、产品和商业决策的汇聚点。替代这一角色比填补组织架构图上的一个方框更难。他说他 [不相信接班人计划](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-12/jensen-huang-nvidia-ceo-succession-plan-after-spy-plot/107376722)。

![](https://i0.wp.com/steveblank.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/nvidia-org-chart.jpg?w=1430&ssl=1)

> When visionary founders depart (resignation, death, firing, etc.), their operating executives believe it’s their turn to run the company. At Microsoft, Bill Gates chose Steve Ballmer, and at Apple Steve Jobs chose Tim Cook. Jensen anointed no one.

当有有远见的创始人离开公司（辞职、去世、被解雇等）时，他们的运营高管认为，是轮到他们来经营公司了。在微软，比尔·盖茨选择了史蒂夫·巴尔默；在苹果，史蒂夫·乔布斯选择了蒂姆·库克。Jensen 没有钦定任何人。

> Once in charge, one of the first things these operations/execution CEOs do is to get rid of the chaos and turbulence in the organization. Execution CEOs value stability, process and repeatable execution. On one hand that’s great for predictability, but it often starts a *creative death spiral* – creative people start to leave, and other executors (without the innovation talent of the old leader) are put into more senior roles – hiring more process people, which in turn forces out the remaining creative talent. This culture shift ripples down from the top and *what once felt like a company on a mission to change the world now feels like another job*.

一旦掌权，这些运营型或执行型CEO的首要任务之一就是消除组织内部的混乱和动荡。执行型CEO重视稳定、流程和可重复的执行，这无疑有助于提高可预测性，但往往会导致“创造力死亡螺旋”：创意人才开始离去，而缺乏创新能力的其他执行者则被提拔到更高级别，招聘更多流程型人才，这反过来又迫使剩余的创意人才离开。这种文化转变从上至下蔓延，曾经令人热血沸腾的“改变世界”的公司，如今却让人感到不过是另一份平凡的工作。

> (Amazon is a counterexample. Before succeeding Jeff Bezos, Andy Jassy had already [founded and led AWS](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/officers-and-directors/person-details/default.aspx?ItemId=be0a9875-4456-4f84-b4d9-ec2e4b97d1d8). He was an innovator who had built a new business inside the existing company.)

（Amazon 则是一个反例。在 Jeff Bezos 之前接任的 Andy Jassy 早已[创建并领导了 AWS](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/officers-and-directors/person-details/default.aspx?ItemId=be0a9875-4456-4f84-b4d9-ec2e4b97d1d8)。他是一位在既有公司内部打造出全新业务的创新者。）

> At Nvidia this step isn’t optional. No one else can run a company with 60 direct reports, so the next CEO’s first move will be to add layers of management.

在英特尔，这一步并非可选。然而，没有人能够管理一个拥有60名直接下属的公司，因此下一任CEO的第一个行动将是增加管理层级。

> **Why Tim Cook Was the New Steve Ballmer  
> **Tim Cook ran Apple for 15 years. Apple under Cook grew its revenues from $108 billion to $416 billion and its market value from $350 billion to about $4 trillion. The iPhone continued its annual upgrades of incremental improvements. The transition to Apple silicon showed that major technical change on the chip level was possible under his leadership. But Apple silicon was a better engine for the existing products Apple already had, not a new market.

**为什么蒂姆·库克是新的史蒂夫·鲍尔默**

蒂姆·库克执掌苹果 15 年。在库克领导下，苹果的营收从 1080 亿美元增至 4160 亿美元，市值从 3500 亿美元增至约 4 万亿美元。iPhone 依然保持着每年一次的渐进式改进升级。向 Apple silicon 的转型表明，在他的领导下，芯片层面的重大技术变革是可行的。但 Apple silicon 只是为苹果现有产品提供了一个更好的引擎，而不是开辟了一个新市场。

> Apple stumbled badly on the one major product category launched under Cook – [Apple Vision Pro](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/04/29/apple-vision-pro-m5-flop/) and missed badly on AI (Siri was actually first with a Chatbot.) This year it turned to Google to help fix Siri. In September 2026 Cook handed the job to John Ternus.

库克任内推出的唯一一个重大产品品类 [Apple Vision Pro](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/04/29/apple-vision-pro-m5-flop/) 遭遇了严重失利，在 AI 方面也大败而归（其实 Siri 才是最早推出聊天机器人的）。今年，它转而求助于 Google 来修复 Siri。2026 年 9 月，库克将这项工作交给了 John Ternus。

> **How Nvidia Could Miss the Boat  
> **The threat to Nvidia is already visible: its biggest customers are becoming its competitors. Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta, OpenAI and Tesla are all [building their own AI chips](https://techcentral.co.za/nvidias-best-customers-are-becoming-its-biggest-threat/285506/). Jensen’s answer was to keep innovating and bring to market a new generation of chips every year, build a moat around an integrated hardware and software platform ([CUDA](https://developer.nvidia.com/cuda/toolkit)), and invest ~$50 billion in the AI labs that run on Nvidia.

**Nvidia 如何可能错失良机**

Nvidia 面临的威胁已经显而易见：其最大的客户正在变成其竞争对手。Google、Amazon、Microsoft、Meta、OpenAI 和 Tesla 都在[打造自己的 AI 芯片](https://techcentral.co.za/nvidias-best-customers-are-becoming-its-biggest-threat/285506/)。Jensen 的应对之策是持续创新，每年推出新一代芯片，围绕软硬件一体化平台（[CUDA](https://developer.nvidia.com/cuda/toolkit)）构建护城河，并向基于 Nvidia 运行的 AI 实验室投资约 500 亿美元。

> It’s likely these are the bets an execution CEO trims first.

执行型首席执行官最可能首先削减的，就是这些押注。

> *The irony is that in the 21st century, the tighter you hold on to your current product/markets, the likelier you will be disrupted*. The rapid change of technology will create new disruptions that will force “bet the company” decisions about product direction, markets, pricing, supply chain, operations and the reorganization necessary to execute a new business model. In the GPU market, the difference between market dominance and Chapter 11 may be a hands-on product/customer, and business model-centric CEO with an entrepreneurial vision of the future.

*讽刺的是，在21世纪，你对现有产品和市场的掌控越紧密，就越有可能被颠覆。*技术的快速变革将催生新的颠覆性力量，迫使企业就产品方向、市场、价格、供应链、运营以及执行新商业模式所需的重组做出“押公司”的决定。在GPU市场，市场主导地位与破产（Chapter 11）之间的差异，可能就在于一位以产品和客户为中心的、以业务模式为核心、拥有未来创业愿景的CEO。

> **Four Challenges for the Board of Directors  
> **The dilemma facing the board at Nvidia or any board of directors on the departure of an innovative CEO is *strategic*: Do we still want to be an innovative, risk taking company? Or should we now focus on execution of our core business, reduce our risky bets and maximize shareholder return. Ultimately a board would like both – preserving the speed and ambition of Jensen’s decision-making while distributing enough authority for the company to function without him.

**董事会的四大挑战**
**英伟达或任何公司董事会在创新型CEO离任时都会面临一个战略性难题：我们是否仍希望保持创新精神和冒风险的态度？还是应该专注于核心业务的执行，减少高风险投资，并最大化股东回报？董事会最终希望同时实现这两个目标——既要保持詹姆斯（Jensen）的决策速度和远大目标，又要分配足够的权力，让公司能够在没有他的情况下继续运营。**

> *Tactically*, that question results in asking: Do you search for another innovator from outside, promote one of the executors or go deeper down the organization to find an innovator?

*Tactically* 该问题结果是在问：你是搜索另一个外部的创新者，提拔执行者之一，还是深入组织内部去寻找创新者？

> Herein lies four challenges.

此处存在四大挑战。

> 1. Steve Jobs and Bill Gates chose execution executives as their successors. The result was they confused world-class execution with the passion for product and customers, and market insight. From the perspective of Gates there was no difference between him and Ballmer and Jobs believed Cook could be equally creative. Yet history has shown they were world-class stewards of a business model each had been handed. Yet *long-term* survival in markets that change rapidly require innovation and execution..

1. Steve Jobs 和 Bill Gates 选择将执行官作为他们的继任者。结果是，他们混淆了世界级执行力与对产品和客户以及市场洞察的热情。从 Bill Gates 的角度来看，他和 Ballmer 之间没有区别，而 Jobs 认为 Cook 也能同样有创造力。然而历史表明，他们各自都是对自己手中交给他们的商业模式的世界级管理者。然而，在那些变化迅速的市场中，*long-term* 的生存需要创新和执行.

> Nvidia’s board has a different version of this problem. Jensen suggested no one, and he left the board the day he left the job. The decision belongs to a board in crisis, led by the first chair the company has ever had. Boards in crisis reach for stability. That gets you an execution CEO by a different road. Nvidia’s board should ask what its candidates have built under uncertainty.
> 2. If the board decides that the company needs another innovator at the helm, you can almost guarantee that the best executor – the number 2 and/or 3 vice president in the company – will leave, feeling that they deserved the job. Now the board is faced with not only having lost its CEO, but potentially the best of the executive staff.
> 3. Many innovative/visionary CEOs have become part of the company’s brand. Steve Jobs, Mark Zuckerberg, Elon Musk and Jensen Huang. This isn’t a new phenomenon, think of 20th-century icons like Walt Disney, Edwin Land at Polaroid and Henry Ford. But they’re not only an external face to the company, they were often the touchstone for *internal* decision-making. Years after a visionary CEO is gone, companies are still asking, “What would Walt Disney/Steve Jobs/Henry Ford have done?” rather than figuring out what *they* should now be doing in the changing market.

Nvidia 的董事会面临不同版本的这个问题。Jensen 建议不任他人，他离职的当天也随即离开了董事会。决定权属于一家陷入危机的董事会，该董事会由公司历史上第一位董事长领导。处于危机的董事会会寻求稳定。这条路让你得到一位执行型 CEO。Nvidia 的董事会应该询问其候选人在不确定性中建立了什么。

2. 如果董事会决定公司需要另一位创新者掌舵，你几乎可以保证，公司最好的 executor – number 2 and/or 3 vice president in the company – 会离开，感到他们理应得到这份工作。现在，董事会不仅面临失去 CEO，还可能失去公司最好的高管团队。

3. 许多创新/远见卓识的 CEO 已成为公司的 brand 一部分。Steve Jobs, Mark Zuckerberg, Elon Musk and Jensen Huang。这并非新现象，举个例子，还有 20th-century 如 Walt Disney, Edwin Land at Polaroid and Henry Ford 等图标。但他们不只是公司的外部形象， 还是*internal*决策的基石。一位创新 CEO 被替换后多年，公司仍在问，“What would Walt Disney/Steve Jobs/Henry Ford have done？”而不是想一想 *they* 应该现在在变化的市场里做什么。

> Jensen was more than the brand. He set Nvidia’s foreign policy, and was the company’s face in Washington, Beijing and Taipei. No Nvidia operating VP has done that job. The next Nvidia CEO inherits a Congress and investigators asking how many restricted Nvidia chips reached China.
> 4. As companies grow larger and management falls prey to the fallacy that it only exists to maximize shareholder *short-term* return on investment, companies become risk averse. Large companies and their boards live in fear of losing what they spent years gaining (customers, market share, revenue, profits.) This may work in stable markets and technologies. But today very few of those remain. And certainly not Nvidia’s market.

黄仁勋不仅仅是一个品牌。他制定了英伟达的外交政策，也是该公司在华盛顿、北京和台北的代言人。英伟达没有任何一位运营副总裁承担过这项工作。下一任英伟达 CEO 接手时，将面对国会和调查人员质询：有多少受限的英伟达芯片流入了中国。
