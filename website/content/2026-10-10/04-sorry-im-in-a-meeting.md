会议音频：职场自我防御——对抗偷时间的行为。挑选一次会议，按下播放，让背景噪音来发表意见。

> ## How it works

## 工作原理

> What is this?
> Fleeting plays long, recordings of made-up meetings. Put it on speaker in the background and anyone nearby will assume you're busy.


Fleeting 播放长时间的虚构会议录音。将其作为背景音外放，附近的任何人都会以为你正忙。

> Will it loop obviously?

它会显然地 loop 吗？

> Each meeting is about twelve minutes long and starts at a random point, so you won't hear the same opening line twice in a row.

每次会议大约持续十二分钟，随机开始，因此你不会连续两次听到相同的开场白。

> How do I end the meeting convincingly?

我如何令人信服的结束会议？

> Press "Leave" or "I have to drop". The other participants say goodbye before the call ends.

点击 "Leave" 或 "I have to drop"。其他参与者在通话结束前说再见。

> What happens when a meeting runs out?


会议时间到了会发生什么？

> With "Back-to-back meetings" ticked, once you've heard the whole meeting everyone says goodbye and, a few seconds later, you join another one that suits the time of day. Untick it and the meeting loops instead.


当“Back-to-back meetings”被勾选时，听完整个会议后，每个人都会说再见，几秒钟后，你会加入另一个适合当前时间的会议。如果取消勾选，会议就会循环。


> Are these real people?

这些是真实的人吗

> No. Every voice is synthetic and the faces are AI-generated or licensed stock footage. Every name is invented except John Carroll, who made the site and sometimes sits in with his camera off. No real meetings are recorded.

不。每个声音都是 synthetic 且面部为 AI-generated 或 licensed stock footage。每个名字都是编造的，除了 John Carroll，他制作了该站点，有时会参与但摄像头关闭。不记录真实会议。

> How do I hide the address bar?

如何隐藏地址栏?

> On a computer the call goes full screen when you join (press F to toggle). On a phone, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" option and open Fleeting from there. It then runs full screen like an app.

在电脑上，调用进入全屏模式时（按 F 键可切换）。在手机上，请使用浏览器的“添加到主屏幕”选项，从 Fleeting 打开即可。随后它会以应用的形式运行全屏。
