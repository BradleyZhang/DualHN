> ## Peeking Through the Keyhole

## 透过钥匙孔窥视

> An [internal ICE document](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.mnd.234416/gov.uscourts.mnd.234416.212.1_5.pdf) laying out the scope of Operation Puppet Master—which led to the indictment of the Minneapolis 15—ends by noting, “This case has also been accepted under CUC Op Keyhole.” According to [prosecutors](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.mnd.234416/gov.uscourts.mnd.234416.263.0_1.pdf), Operation Keyhole is a long-standing program created by HSI’s Saint Paul office to fund “undercover activity”—primarily equipment purchases and personnel costs—in a region covering Minnesota, North and South Dakota, Iowa, and Nebraska. The program has funded at least 77 investigations into “narcotics trafficking, child sexual abuse, financial investigations, and counter-proliferation” since it started in December 2008.

一个 [internal ICE document](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.mnd.234416/gov.uscourts.mnd.234416.212.1_5.pdf) 详细列出了 Operation Puppet Master 的范围——该行动导致了 Minneapolis 15 的起诉——并在结尾指出，“此案也已被接受 under CUC Op Keyhole。” 根据 [检察官](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.mnd.234416/gov.uscourts.mnd.234416.263.0_1.pdf)，Operation Keyhole 是由 HSI 的 Saint Paul 办公室创建的一个长期项目，用于资助“伪装活动”——主要包括设备采购和人员费用——在 覆盖 Minnesota, North and South Dakota, Iowa, and Nebraska 的地区。该项目自 2008 年 12 月成立以来，已资助了至少 77 起针对 “毒品走私、儿童性虐待、财务调查和扩散防范” 的调查。

> The CUC prefix, per an internal 2008 DHS handbook [unearthed by the independent media outlet Unicorn Riot](https://media.unicornriot.ninja/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/ice-undercover-operations.pdf), refers to Certified Undercover Operations, a formal designation with clearly defined oversight mechanisms, procedures, and reporting requirements. Operation Keyhole’s parameters are reviewed every six months by a committee comprising senior DOJ and DHS officials. As of June 2026, when it was last renewed, Keyhole funded six active investigations, including Project Whipple Shield, which was initiated in February to probe claims of assault, stalking, and doxing of DHS agents and other federal employees, and underpins the Minnesota 15 indictment.

根据内部 2008 年 DHS 手册（由独立媒体 Unicorn Riot [发掘](https://media.unicornriot.ninja/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/ice-undercover-operations.pdf)），CUC prefix 指的是 Certified Undercover Operations，即一项具有明确界定的监督机制、程序和报告要求的正式指定。Operation Keyhole 的参数由一个由资深 DOJ 和 DHS 官员组成的委员会每六个月审查一次。截至 2026 年 6 月（最近一次续期时），Keyhole 资助了六项活跃调查，包括 Project Whipple Shield。该项目于 2 月启动，旨在调查 DHS 特工及其他联邦雇员遭受袭击、跟踪和 doxing 的指控，并构成了 Minnesota 15 起诉的基础。

> At least 11 undercover agents working under Operation Keyhole infiltrated Twin Cities anti-ICE activists, participating in martial arts and know-your-rights trainings, marches, general strikes, and community meetings. They also infiltrated activists’ communications network for identifying possible undercover ICE vehicles and dispatching activists to tail them through the Twin Cities, and penetrated several activist group chats on Signal, transmitting their contents back to other HSI colleagues for analysis and eventual inclusion in the indictment.

至少 11 名卧底特工在 Operation Keyhole 下工作，渗透至 Twin Cities 地区的反 ICE 活动人士，参与了武术和知情权培训、游行、总罢工以及社区会议。他们还渗透了活动人士的通信网络，以识别可能的卧底 ICE 车辆，并派遣活动人士在 Twin Cities 地区尾随这些车辆；随后，他们渗透了 Signal 上几组活动人士群聊，将聊天内容转发给其他 HSI 同事，供分析，并最终纳入起诉。

> Activist conferences as far away as Seattle, Michigan, [Chicago](https://www.wired.com/story/chicago-area-cops-helped-hsi-spy-on-minnesota-anti-ice-activists/), and New York City were placed under surveillance as part of the investigation, and HSI teams photographed and ran the license plates of every car parked in the lot of a meeting location or seen transporting activists. There is also drone footage from unarmed Customs and Border Protection Reaper drones of the activists moving around the Twin Cities area.



作为调查的一部分，远至西雅图、密歇根、[Chicago](https://www.wired.com/story/chicago-area-cops-helped-hsi-spy-on-minnesota-anti-ice-activists/) 和纽约市的活动人士会议均受到了监视。HSI 团队拍摄并查询了停在会议地点停车场或被发现运送活动人士的每辆车的车牌。还有来自无武装的 Customs and Border Protection Reaper 无人机的无人机镜头，记录了活动人士在双城地区走动的画面。

> The undercovers were part of a much broader group of more than 50 HSI agents, including at least two dozen from HSI’s little-known Special Operations Group, that took part in the investigation and surveillance of the defendants, both digital and in-person. Feds with decades of experience on complex financial crimes, drug cartel investigations, human trafficking, and organized crime were pulled into the Minnesota 15 investigation.

这些卧底特工属于一个规模大得多的团队，该团队由 50 多名 HSI 特工组成，其中包括至少 24 名来自 HSI 鲜为人知的特别行动组 的成员，他们参与了对被告的调查与监视，涵盖线上与线下两种方式。多名在复杂金融犯罪、毒品卡特尔调查、人口贩运及有组织犯罪领域拥有数十年经验的联邦特工，被调入“明尼苏达 15 案”调查中。

> Several of the Minnesota 15 case agents are from out of state and appear in court records for the prosecutions of anti-ICE activists in other cities. This includes Los Angeles–based HSI agents [Robert Kurtz III](https://www.nacdl.org/getattachment/6e8dd2ce-b13a-43c3-87d6-5935837a1618/gov-uscourts-cacd-988767-1-0.pdf), who specializes in financial fraud, and Ryan Ribner, who allegedly [assaulted](https://emptywheel.net/2026/01/12/dhs-assaulting-protesters-because-goons-believe-they-are-vicious-horrible-people/) SEIU California president David Huerta at a June 2025 protest of an ICE enforcement operation, arrested him, and then claimed the then 58-year-old Huerta attacked federal law enforcement. (Huerta’s charges were [dropped](https://www.courthousenews.com/justice-department-drops-criminal-charge-against-labor-leader-david-huerta/) in July after videos contradicting Ribner’s account surfaced.) HSI records show Ribner was also involved in at least two prosecutions of Southern California residents who physically confronted ICE and Border Patrol agents last year.



明尼苏达 15 案的部分办案人员来自州外，并出现在其他城市起诉反 ICE 活动人士的法庭记录中。这包括驻洛杉矶的 HSI 特工 [Robert Kurtz III](https://www.nacdl.org/getattachment/6e8dd2ce-b13a-43c3-87d6-5935837a1618/gov-uscourts-cacd-988767-1-0.pdf)（专攻金融欺诈）和 Ryan Ribner。据称，Ryan Ribner 在 2025 年 6 月一次 ICE 执法行动的抗议活动中 `[袭击了](https://emptywheel.net/2026/01/12/dhs-assaulting-protesters-because-goons-believe-they-are-vicious-horrible-people/)` SEIU California 主席 David Huerta，将其逮捕，并随后声称当时 58 岁的 Huerta 袭击了联邦执法人员。（在出现与 Ribner 的说法相矛盾的视频后，对 Huerta 的指控于 7 月 `[被撤销](https://www.courthousenews.com/justice-department-drops-criminal-charge-against-labor-leader-david-huerta/)`)。HSI 记录显示，Ryan Ribner 还参与了至少两起对南加州居民的起诉，这些居民去年与 ICE 和 Border Patrol 特工发生了肢体冲突。

> Other agents from the conspiracy investigation have also been involved in recent high-profile incidents. Tim Gerber, the “team leader” of an HSI Special Operations Group squad, led the investigation of former CNN host Don Lemon for reporting on a protest at a Minneapolis Church where an ICE agent was a pastor. Fellow SOG agent Yue Her was present at the scene of a 2025 incident when Renee Good’s killer, ICE Special Response Team agent Johnathan Ross, was dragged by the car of a man he tried to apprehend.

其他来自阴谋调查的特工也参与了近期几起高调事件。Tim Gerber, “团队领袖” 的 HSI Special Operations Group 小组领导了对前 CNN 主持人 Don Lemon 的调查，后者因在 Minneapolis Church （明尼阿波利斯教堂）一场抗议中报道一名 ICE 代理人担任牧师的情况而受到关注。Fellow SOG 成员 Yue Her 在 2025 年 事件现场出现，当时 Renee Good 的凶手，ICE Special Response Team 成员 Johnathan Ross，被一名他试图逮捕的男子的车辆拖行。

> “This operation does not seem in any way limited to Minnesota,” says Spencer Reynolds, a former DHS attorney and counterterrorism lawyer who now works at the NAACP Legal Defense Fund. “As we saw with the boots on the ground in the streets, with Greg Bovino leading CBP and Border Patrol special forces units on sweeping raids across the country, this is the intelligence and undercover investigative apparatus that is running in parallel to the door-kicking.”

“这项行动似乎并不受限于 Minnesota”， says Spencer Reynolds, a former DHS attorney and counterterrorism lawyer who now works at the NAACP Legal Defense Fund. “正如我们在地面上看到的，随着 Greg Bovino 领导 CBP 和 Border Patrol 特种部队在全国范围内进行扫荡行动，这是运行在 parallel door-kicking 之外的情报和卧底调查 apparatus.”

> ## Follow the Money

## 追随资金

> Mike German, a retired FBI agent who worked undercover domestic terrorism cases for years before becoming a sharp critic of post-9/11 federal law enforcement, likens the Minnesota 15 case to federal law enforcement’s long history of suppressing left-wing social movements, including the [CIA’s Operation MH/Chaos](https://embed.documentcloud.org/documents/238963-huge-c-i-a-operation-reported-in-u-s-against/), which surveilled the anti-war movement during the Vietnam War for evidence of foreign influence. One difference is the sheer scale—this time, it’s bigger.

Mike German，前FBI特工，多年从事反间谍 domestic terrorism 案件侦查，后成为对 9/11 之后联邦执法机构的批评人。他将明密尼15案与联邦执法机构长期以来镇压左翼社会运动的历史进行了类比，其中包括 [CIA 的 MH/Chaos 操作](https://embed.documentcloud.org/documents/238963-huge-c-i-a-operation-reported-in-u-s-against/)，该行动在越战期间对反战运动展开监视，以寻找外国影响的蛛丝马迹。不同之处在于规模之大——这一次，规模更大。

> According to a [2011 book by former CIA officer Frank Rafalko](https://www.abebooks.com/Mhchaos-CIAs-Campaign-Against-Radical-New/32279501056/bd), the CIA secret investigation into suspected foreign influence on the New Left and anti-Vietnam War movement had 42 officers working the case at its peak. Court records show 119 HSI agents worked on the Minnesota 15 case alone.

根据 [2011 年由前 CIA 官员 Frank Rafalko 撰写的一本书](https://www.abebooks.com/Mhchaos-CIAs-Campaign-Against-Radical-New/32279501056/bd) 所述，CIA 秘密调查怀疑有外界影响介入新左派和反越南战争运动，在调查达到顶峰时，共有 42 名官员在处理该案。法庭记录显示，仅在明尼苏达 15 案中，就有 119 名 HSI 特工在处理该案。

> “Broad monitoring of social media, communications, and financial transactions are used to link an ever increasing number of people as potential subjects of investigation and prosecution,” German says. “These are exactly the kind of intelligence activities J. Edgar Hoover adopted to suppress the Civil Rights and anti-war movements in the 1960s.”

“广泛的监控社交媒体、通讯和金融交易被用来链接越来越多的人作为潜在的调查和起诉主体，”German 说。“这些正是 J. Edgar Hoover 在 1960 年代 采用的镇压民权和反战运动的那类情报活动。”

> The key difference today, German says, is the vast pool of data available to the feds.

德国人说，今天的关键不同之处在于联邦政府可获得的海量数据池。

> HSI used highly invasive measures—including scrutiny of wire transfers, PayPal reimbursements, and other financial transactions; forensic digital analysis of website traffic; 15 terabytes worth of infiltrated Signal chats; and information from a commercial data broker—to identify individuals under investigation.



HSI 采用了高度侵入性的措施——包括对电汇、PayPal 退款及其他金融交易的审查；对网站流量的数字取证分析；渗透获取的 15 TB Signal 聊天数据；以及来自商业数据经纪商的信息——以识别受调查的个人。

> After a local activist uploaded a license-plate search tool on Fly.io, a developer-focused public cloud platform, to an infiltrated Signal chat, HSI agents reviewed website [DNS queries and Stripe data](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/28831278-govuscourtsmnd2344162313-5/) along with data purchased from a commercial broker. This gave HSI access to SSH session logs, outbound connection records, the Fly.io account’s signup IP, and a Stripe card fingerprint on an account that had never completed a payment. Agents fused that with Minnesota DVS records and commercial data from the Thomson-Reuters CLEAR platform to produce a driver's license number, Social Security number summary, years of address history, phone numbers, property records, and jointly registered vehicles.
