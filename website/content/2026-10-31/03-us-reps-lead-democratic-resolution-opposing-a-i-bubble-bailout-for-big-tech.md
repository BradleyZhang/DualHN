> **Washington, D.C.** — Today, Congressional Progressive Caucus (CPC) Chair Rep. Greg Casar (TX-35) and House Financial Services Committee Ranking Member Rep. Maxine Waters (CA-43) co-led the introduction of a resolution warning of the economic risks posed by an artificial-intelligence-related asset bubble, opposing a bailout of corporations or their business executives impacted by the collapse of an A.I. bubble, and affirming their support for aggressive federal action to protect workers, retirees, and low-income communities who will be harmed by an economic downturn.

**华盛顿特区** — 今天，国会进步党团（CPC）主席众议员格雷格·卡萨尔（TX-35）与众议院金融服务委员会少数党Ranking Member众议员马克西·沃特斯（CA-43）共同提出一项决议，警告人工智能相关资产泡沫带来的经济风险，反对对因人工智能泡沫破裂而受影响的公司或其业务负责人进行救助，并声明支持采取强力联邦措施保护工人、退休人员和低收入社区，因为他们将受到经济 downturn 的伤害。

> They were joined by Representatives Danny Davis (IL-07), Chris Deluzio (PA-17), Valerie P. Foushee (NC-04), Jesús “Chuy” García (IL-04), Pramila Jayapal (WA-07), Analilia Mejia (NJ-11), Delia Ramirez (IL-03), André Carson (IN-07), Jan Schakowsky (IL-09), Brad Sherman (CA-32), and Rashida Tlaib (MI-12).

他们还被代表人 Danny Davis（IL-07）、Chris Deluzio（PA-17）、Valerie P. Foushee（NC-04）、Jesús "Chuy" García（IL-04）、Pramila Jayapal（WA-07）、Analilia Mejia（NJ-11）、Delia Ramirez（IL-03）、André Carson（IN-07）、Jan Schakowsky（IL-09）、Brad Sherman（CA-32）和 Rashida Tlaib（MI-12）加入。

> “Big Tech billionaires are making increasingly reckless bets on A.I. They got richer off Trump’s deregulation and Republican tax breaks. Now they expect taxpayers to bail them out if those bets go bad. We won’t let them," said CPC Chair Casar. "A.I. companies that fail should go bankrupt, and executives who commit fraud should be held accountable. Federal help should go to workers and retirees.”

“大型科技巨头亿万富翁们正在 A.I. 领域做出越来越鲁莽的赌注。他们从特朗普的放宽管制和 Republican 减税中变得更加富有。现在他们期望纳税人在赌注失败时救助他们。我们不会允许，”CPC 主席 Casar 说。“失败的 A.I. 公司应该破产，犯有欺诈行为的高管应受到追究。联邦援助应给予工人和退休人员。”

> “Big tech and Wall Street are taking enormous risks with billions in borrowed money to fuel the AI boom, despite growing warning signs of a dangerous bubble and if it all comes crashing down they should not expect taxpayers to rescue them,” said Ranking Member Waters. “We saw what happened in 2008 when reckless Wall Street practices brought our economy to its knees. The banks got bailed out while millions of hardworking Americans lost their jobs, homes, and retirement savings. We cannot let history repeat itself. The American people should never again be forced to foot the bill for billionaires’ reckless bets.”

大科技和 Wall Street 正以巨额借款燃料 AI 浪潮，尽管日益增多的危险泡沫警示，一旦一切崩塌，他们不应指望纳税人救援，” Ranking Member Waters 说。“我们在 2008 年目睹了当鲁莽的 Wall Street 做法把我们的经济拖入谷底时的情景。当时银行被救助，而数以百万计的美国人失去了工作、房屋和退休储蓄。我们绝不能让历史重演。美国人民不应再被迫为亿万富翁的鲁莽投注买单。

> In the resolution, the Democratic lawmakers note that “artificial intelligence-related firms hold trillions of dollars in debt, much of which is raised in opaque and unregulated private credit markets,” and “the price-to-earnings ratio of the stock market is currently almost as high as it was at the peak of the 1990s tech bubble.”



在该决议中，民主党议员指出，“人工智能相关公司持有数万亿美元的债务，其中大部分是在不透明且不受监管的私人信贷市场筹集的”，并且“股票市场的市盈率目前几乎与 1990 年代科技泡沫巅峰期一样高。”

> The Democratic legislators also point out that “federal regulators have avoided applying robust scrutiny and oversight regarding enormously complicated debt and equity schemes used by artificial intelligence firms” and highlight the Trump White House’s deregulatory focus and conflicts of interest related to A.I.

民主立法者还指出，“联邦监管者对人工智能公司使用的极其复杂的债务和股权方案缺乏强有力的审查和监管”，并强调特朗普白宫的去监管重点以及与人工智能相关的利益冲突。

> The resolution’s cosponsors commit to avoiding a federal response similar to what occurred during the financial crisis of 2008, where the financial sector received trillions of dollars in liquidity without accountability for senior executives involved in illegal and fraudulent behaviors. Meanwhile, 10 million homeowners lost their houses and tens of millions of workers went years without adequate employment while too-big-to-fail banks enjoyed a massive bailout.

决议的联合发起人承诺避免重复像2008年金融危机那样的联邦政府响应，当时金融部门获得了数万亿美元的流动性支持，但却没有对参与非法和欺诈行为的高管们进行任何问责。与此同时，1000万个家庭失去了自己的房产，数千万的工人们在失业或工作不足的情况下度过了多年时光，而那些“太大而不能失败”的银行却享受到了巨额的救助。

> The resolution urges the Federal Government, including Congress and key Federal agencies to oppose:

决议促请联邦政府，包括国会和关键联邦机构，反对：

> - Any measure to rescue insolvent artificial intelligence-related firms or to shield their senior executives from accountability for reckless conduct;
> - Special assistance like emergency lending or direct capital infusions to prop up insolvent A.I. companies’ operations or prevent their orderly resolutions through ordinary bankruptcy processes;
> - “Golden parachutes” for corporate executives involved in risky or illegal behaviors that lead to their firms’ insolvency, instead requiring them to be held fully accountable under civil and criminal law and be obligated to return ill-gotten gains like mansions, vacation homes, private planes and yachts, and other assets.

- 任何拯救 insolvent 人工智能相关公司或免除其高层高管对鲁莽行为的问责责任；
- 如紧急借贷或直接资本注入，以支撑 insolvent A.I. 公司的运营，或阻止其通过普通破产程序的有序化解；
- “Golden parachutes” 涉及使公司破产的风险或非法行为的企业高管，而是要求他们依据民事和刑事法律被全面追究责任，并被迫归还非法获益，如豪宅、度假别墅、私人飞机和游艇以及其他资产。

> The resolution also calls on the Federal Government to support:

决议还呼吁联邦政府支持：

> - Aggressive, direct federal action to support ordinary Americans through federal spending to create jobs, protect state and local government services, and robust unemployment insurance, nutrition support, and housing assistance;
> - The protection of pension and retirement fund holders who may face devastating losses in the event of a collapse of an A.I. bubble;
> - Far-reaching reforms to prevent future speculative bubbles, including additional transparency and accountability requirements;
> - Automatic economic stabilizers—investments in people that start to flow immediately in the event of an economic downturn—that support workers and consumers when financial crises, asset bubbles and other harms create downturns and recessions.

- 积极、直接的联邦行动，通过联邦支出创造就业岗位、保护州和地方政府服务，以及提供健全的失业保险、营养支持和住房援助，以支持普通美国民众；
- 保护养老金和退休基金持有者，防止他们在 A.I. 泡沫破裂时面临毁灭性损失；
- 深远的改革以防止未来的投机性泡沫，包括额外的透明度和问责要求；
- 自动经济稳定器——在经济低迷时立即启动的对人的投资——当金融危机、资产泡沫和其他危害造成衰退和经济萧条时，支持工人和消费者。

> The resolution was endorsed by American Federation of Teachers, American Economic Liberties Project, Americans for Financial Reform, the Center for Responsible Lending, Demand Progress Action, Future of Life Institute, Guardrails Action, Indivisible, Interfaith Centre on Corporate Responsibility, Investor Alliance for Human Rights, Jobs With Justice, P Street, and Social Security Works.

该决议获得了 American Federation of Teachers、American Economic Liberties Project、Americans for Financial Reform、the Center for Responsible Lending、Demand Progress Action、Future of Life Institute、Guardrails Action、Indivisible、Interfaith Centre on Corporate Responsibility、Investor Alliance for Human Rights、Jobs With Justice、P Street 和 Social Security Works 的支持。

> “Americans are already paying for the AI build-out through higher power bills and a stock market propped up by a handful of companies,” said Morgan Harper, Director of Policy & Advocacy for American Economic Liberties Project. They should not pay a second time with a bailout. When Wall Street’s bets went bad during the financial crisis, the banks got rescued and ordinary people got foreclosure notices. Congressional members are right to reject AI bailouts in advance, and commit to saving real help for those who will be left holding the bag—the American people.”



“美国人已经通过更高的电费账单和由少数几家公司支撑的股市，为 AI 建设付出了代价，”美国经济自由项目 (American Economic Liberties Project) 的政策与倡导总监摩根·哈珀 (Morgan Harper) 表示，“他们不应该再为救助付出第二次代价。在金融危机期间，当华尔街的赌注失败时，银行获得了救助，而普通人却收到了止赎通知。国会议员提前拒绝 AI 救助是正确的，并应该致力于将真正的帮助留给那些最终将承担损失的人——美国人民。”

> “Tech billionaires and their Wall Street backers are using dangerous tactics reminiscent of the 2008 financial crisis to make trillion dollar bets on AI—and they are putting working people’s money at risk,” said Maya Jenkins, a senior policy analyst at Americans for Financial Reform. “This resolution charts a critically important path for defending people’s economic security from the bubble’s blast radius, opposing billionaire bailouts, and holding corporate executives to account. This is the kind of approach needed to confront mounting financial risks and emerge from the aftermath of the AI bubble with an economy that is fairer and more stable.”

“科技亿万富翁及其华尔街支持者正在使用与2008年金融危机类似的危险手段，进行数万亿美元的人工智能赌局，正在将普通民众的资金置于风险之中，”美国金融改革组织资深政策分析师玛雅·詹金斯表示。“该决议为保护民众的经济安全免受泡沫冲击、防止亿万富翁获得救助、追究企业高管责任指明了一条极其重要的路径。这是应对日益增长的金融风险、从人工智能泡沫的后果中走出的必要手段，有助于建立一个更加公平和稳定的经济体。”

> “When the housing market collapsed in 2008, the financial industry was prepared to act to ensure that there was a bailout that benefited them,” said Dean Baker, senior economist at the Center for Economic and Policy Research. “It is important that the Congressional Progressive Caucus is stepping forward to make sure that any bailout from the collapse of the AI bubble is designed to benefit the country as whole, rather than the billionaire AI boys who created the problem in the first place.”

2008 年，住房市场崩溃时，金融业已准备好行动，以确保有一个救助惠及他们。迪恩·贝克，经济与政策研究中心资深经济学家说。重要的是 进步派国会委员会 正在挺身而出，以确保任何 AI 泡沫崩溃的救助都设计为惠及整个国家，而不是那些亿万富翁 AI 青年首先制造问题的。

> “Chair Casar, Ranking Member Waters, and the Progressive Caucus should be praised for this resolution and recognized as being at the tip of the spear in ensuring that AI policy focuses on working-class impacts and isn’t simply left to the desires of the handful of billionaires and their courtesans in Congress, said Maya Handa, Senior Advisor, Guardrails Action. “The AI industry is trying to buy our elections and write their own regulations while its biggest players line up IPOs at record valuations. If this turns into a bubble, working-class Americans must not be forced to pay the tab for big tech CEOs’ recklessness and greed. Simply put: there can be no bailout for big AI execs or their financial enablers.”

卡萨尔主席、少数党首席委员沃特斯和进步党团应因这项决议受到赞扬，并被认定为在确保 AI 政策关注工人阶级影响、而不只是由少数几位亿万富翁及其在国会中的随从所支配方面处于矛尖，守护行动高级顾问玛雅·汉达说道。“ AI 行业正试图买通我们的选举并为自己制定法规，而其最大的参与者正在以创纪录的估值排队进行 IPO。如果这变成一个泡沫，工人阶级美国人绝不能被迫为大型科技公司 CEO 的鲁莽和贪婪买单。简单来说：大型 AI 高管及其金融助推者绝不能得到救助。”

> “AI companies and their billionaire executives shouldn’t get to gamble with our economy and democracy, then expect working people to bail them out when their bets on AI go bust,” said Andrew O’Neill, National Advocacy Director of Indivisible. “We learned in 2008 what happens when the government puts impunity for corporate corruption ahead of working families. This resolution draws a clear line: will lawmakers protect AI tech bros or the people they’re supposed to represent?”

“AI 公司和他们的十亿富豪高管不应该赌我们的经济和民主，然后期望普通工人在 AI 投注失败时救助他们，” said Andrew O’Neill, National Advocacy Director of Indivisible. “我们在 2008 年了解到，当政府把对公司腐败的免责置于普通家庭之前时会发生什么，”这项决议划出了明确的界线：议员们会保护 AI tech bros 或者 他们本应代表的人民？

# 使用 LangChain 和 OpenAI 构建文档问答系统

在本教程中，我们将使用 LangChain 和 OpenAI 的 GPT-4 模型构建一个完整的基于文档的问答系统。该系统将允许用户上传 PDF 文档并对其内容提问。

## 先决条件

- Python 编程基础知识
- OpenAI API 密钥
- Python 包：langchain、openai、PyPDF2、streamlit

## 步骤

### 步骤 1：设置项目

创建一个新的 Python 项目并安装所需的包：

```bash
pip install langchain openai PyPDF2 streamlit
```

### 步骤 2：创建文档处理管道

我们将使用 LangChain 创建一个管道，用于：
1. 加载 PDF 文档
2. 将文本分割成块
3. 创建嵌入
4. 将它们存储在向量数据库中

### 步骤 3：构建问答链

使用 LangChain 的 LLMChain 和 RetrievalQA 链：
- 创建提示模板
- 设置检索器
- 配置 LLM（GPT-4）
- 组装链

### 步骤 4：创建用户界面

使用 Streamlit 构建一个 Web 界面，用户可以在其中：
- 上传 PDF 文件
- 输入问题
- 查看答案
